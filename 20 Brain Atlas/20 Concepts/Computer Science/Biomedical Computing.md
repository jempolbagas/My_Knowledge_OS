## The idea (one clear statement)
Biomedical Computing is an interdisciplinary field that leverages computer science, machine learning, signal/image processing, and mathematical modeling to analyze multi-scale biological data and solve medical challenges.

## Why it matters / how it connects
It shifts medicine from reactive observation to predictive, data-driven precision medicine. By connecting micro-level data (DNA/proteins) with macro-level physiological phenomena (organ imaging and bioelectric signals), biomedical computing enables patient-specific simulations like physiological Digital Twins.
