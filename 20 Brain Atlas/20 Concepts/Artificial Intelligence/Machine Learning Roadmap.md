## The idea (one clear statement)
A Machine Learning Roadmap is a self-paced, dependency-based skill tree that guides learners from mathematical and programming foundations (Linear Algebra, Calculus, Python) to classical statistical learning, deep neural networks, and modern specializations (GenAI/LLMs or MLOps).

## Why it matters / how it connects
*   **Dependency-Based Progression:** Hacking or building ML models without understanding linear algebra or vectorized operations leads to fragile abstractions. Structuring learning by prerequisites rather than fixed timelines prevents burnout and encourages curiosity-driven exploration.
*   **Core Milestones:** Moves systematically from implementing basic algorithms (e.g., gradient descent from scratch) to deep learning frameworks (PyTorch, autograd) and production deployment.
