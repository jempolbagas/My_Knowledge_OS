## 📌 Course Objectives & Scope
- Classical ciphers vs modern cryptography principles.
- Symmetric ciphers: Block ciphers (AES, DES), stream ciphers, modes of operation (CBC, GCM).
- Asymmetric cryptography: Modular arithmetic, RSA, Diffie-Hellman, Elliptic Curve Cryptography (ECC).
- Hash functions (SHA-256, SHA-3), Message Authentication Codes (MAC), digital signatures, and PKI.
