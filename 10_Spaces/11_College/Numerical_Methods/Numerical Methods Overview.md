## 📌 Course Objectives & Scope
- Error analysis: Floating-point representation, truncation and round-off errors.
- Root finding for non-linear equations: Bisection, Newton-Raphson, Secant methods.
- Systems of linear equations: Gauss Elimination, LU Decomposition, Gauss-Seidel iteration.
- Polynomial interpolation (Lagrange, Newton), numerical differentiation, and numerical integration (Trapezoidal, Simpson's rules).
