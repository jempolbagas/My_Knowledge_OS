## 📌 Course Objectives & Scope
- Distributed architectures, RPC/RMI, message passing, client-server & P2P models.
- Synchronization: Logical clocks (Lamport, Vector), mutual exclusion, election algorithms.
- Consistency & Replication: CAP Theorem, consensus protocols (Raft, Paxos), eventual consistency.
- Fault tolerance, failure recovery, and distributed storage systems.
