## 📌 Course Objectives & Scope
- Data preprocessing: Cleaning, normalization, feature selection, dimensionality reduction.
- Classification algorithms: Decision Trees, Naive Bayes, KNN, SVM, Ensemble methods.
- Clustering techniques: K-Means, Hierarchical Clustering, DBSCAN.
- Association rule mining (Apriori, FP-Growth) and evaluation metrics (Precision, Recall, ROC-AUC).
