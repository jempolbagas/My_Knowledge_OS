# 🎯 Paket Soal Evaluasi: Perfect Tenses — Standardized Test Package (UTBK & TOEFL)

**Alokasi Waktu:** 45 Menit  
**Jumlah Soal:** 15 Pilihan Ganda (HOTS/UTBK/TOEFL) + 5 Uraian Penalaran  
**Petunjuk:** Pilihlah satu jawaban yang paling tepat untuk soal pilihan ganda, dan berikan penjelasan sistematis untuk soal uraian!

---

## 📝 Part I: Multiple Choice Questions (UTBK / TOEFL Standard)

**1. By the time the delegates arrived at the international summit room, the keynote speaker ______ his speech.**
- A. has already finished
- B. had already finished
- C. will have finished
- D. is finishing
- E. finishes

**2. Dr. Thorne ______ research on renewable solar energy for over fifteen years before he finally won the prestigious energy award last month.**
- A. has conducted
- B. is conducting
- C. had been conducting
- D. conducts
- E. will have conducted

**3. "Can I borrow your economics textbook?" — "I'm sorry, but I ______ it to Sarah, and she hasn't returned it yet."**
- A. lent
- B. have lent
- C. had lent
- D. will lend
- E. lend

**4. By December 2028, the municipal government ______ the construction of the new high-speed subway line connecting the suburbs.**
- A. completes
- B. completed
- C. has completed
- D. will have completed
- E. had completed

**5. Which of the following sentences contains a GRAMMATICAL ERROR?**
- A. We have known each other since we were in primary school.
- B. By next month, she will have been working here for three years.
- C. The student had submitted his essay before the deadline passed.
- D. I have been knowing the secret answer to this puzzle for a long time.
- E. They had not been aware of the updated security regulations.

**6. "Why are you looking so tired and out of breath?" — "Because I ______ the heavy boxes upstairs all morning."**
- A. carry
- B. have been carrying
- C. had carried
- D. will carry
- E. was carried

**7. Identify the underlined part that MUST be changed to make the sentence correct:**  
$\text{"By the time the rescue team}$ $\underset{\text{A}}{\text{reaches}}$ $\text{the remote island, the storm}$ $\underset{\text{B}}{\text{will has destroyed}}$ $\text{most of}$ $\underset{\text{C}}{\text{the coastal infrastructure}}$ $\underset{\text{D}}{\text{and wooden bridges.}}$"
- A. reaches
- B. will has destroyed
- C. the coastal infrastructure
- D. and wooden bridges
- E. No error

**8. She couldn't get into her apartment yesterday evening because she ______ her house keys at the office.**
- A. forgets
- B. has forgotten
- C. had left
- D. is leaving
- E. will have left

**9. Up to the present moment, the committee ______ any formal public statement regarding the sudden policy changes.**
- A. did not issue
- B. has not issued
- C. had not issued
- D. will not issue
- E. is not issuing

**10. By the end of this current decade, autonomous driving technology ______ a ubiquitous sight in major smart cities.**
- A. becomes
- B. became
- C. has become
- D. will have become
- E. had been becoming

**11. Identify the option that best combines these two sentences:**  
*Sentence 1: The chef prepared all the ingredients.*  
*Sentence 2: The guests arrived at the restaurant.*  
*(Note: Preparing ingredients happened FIRST).*
- A. When the chef prepared all the ingredients, the guests have arrived.
- B. The chef had prepared all the ingredients before the guests arrived.
- C. The chef prepared all the ingredients after the guests had arrived.
- D. The guests arrived by the time the chef has prepared all the ingredients.
- E. The chef will have prepared all ingredients when guests arrive.

**12. Mr. Bambang ______ as the principal of SMAN 1 for eight years before he moved to the Ministry of Education in 2024.**
- A. has served
- B. serves
- C. had served
- D. will have served
- E. is serving

**13. "How long ______ in Jakarta before you relocated to Bali?" — "About five years."**
- A. have you lived
- B. had you been living
- C. do you live
- D. will you live
- E. are you living

**14. The scientific report clearly indicates that global sea levels ______ by several centimeters over the past few decades.**
- A. rose
- B. have risen
- C. had risen
- D. will rise
- E. are rising

**15. By 10 PM tonight, the software developers ______ on the new update continuously for twelve hours.**
- A. will work
- B. had worked
- C. have been working
- D. will have been working
- E. worked

## ✍️ Part II: HOTS Essay & Grammar Reasoning Questions

**Soal 1 (Analysis & Error Correction):**  
Jelaskan mengapa kalimat berikut dianggap **SALAH** dalam standar grammar TOEFL/UTBK, lalu berikan kalimat perbaikannya:  
> *"By the time I will graduate from university next year, my parents will have paid off their mortgage."*

**Soal 2 (Contrastive Analysis):**  
Jelaskan perbedaan makna dan implikasi situasi dari kedua kalimat berikut:  
- **Kalimat A:** *Rudi lived in Yogyakarta for 4 years.*
- **Kalimat B:** *Rudi has lived in Yogyakarta for 4 years.*

**Soal 3 (Stative Verb Rule):**  
Mengapa kalimat *"I have been owning this car since 2020"* tidak berterima dalam Bahasa Inggris baku? Aturan apakah yang dilanggar, dan bagaimana bentuk kalimat yang benar?

**Soal 4 (Tense Sequence in Past Narrative):**  
Susun kembali tiga klausa berikut menjadi satu kalimat majemuk berurutan yang logis menggunakan kata hubung *after* dan *before* serta tenses yang tepat:  
- (A) *The fire department extinguished the fire.*
- (B) *The fire burned three local shops.*
- (C) *The police conducted an investigation.*

**Soal 5 (Future Deadline Modeling):**  
Buatlah sebuah paragraf pendek (3-4 kalimat) yang menceritakan rencana karir atau akademismu di tahun 2030 dengan mengintegrasikan sedikitnya **dua penggunaan Future Perfect Tense (`will have + V3`)** dan **satu kata hubung `by the time`**!

## 🔑 Part III: Kunci Jawaban, Pembahasan Lengkap, & Rubrik Penilaian

### 🎯 Kunci Jawaban Part I (Pilihan Ganda)

| No | Jawaban | Pembahasan Line-by-Line |
| :-: | :-: | :--- |
| **1** | **B** | Indikator `By the time + V2 (arrived)` menunjukkan dua kejadian lampau. Kejadian yang selesai lebih dulu (*speaker selesai pidato*) wajib menggunakan **Past Perfect (`had + V3`)**. |
| **2** | **C** | Terdapat penanda durasi lampau (`for over 15 years`) sebelum kejadian lampau kedua (*won the award*). Penekanan durasi lampau menggunakan **Past Perfect Continuous (`had been conducting`)**. |
| **3** | **B** | Kalimat menyatakan aksi lampau yang dampaknya terasa *sekarang* (buku belum kembali sekarang). Gunakan **Present Perfect (`have lent`)**. |
| **4** | **D** | Penanda waktu `By December 2028` (masa depan) mewajibkan penggunaan **Future Perfect (`will have completed`)**. |
| **5** | **D** | Kata kerja *know* adalah **Stative Verb** yang tidak boleh dijadikan bentuk `-ing`. Harusnya: *I have known*. |
| **6** | **B** | Menanyakan aktivitas berdurasi yang baru saja berhenti dan buktinya terasa sekarang (*looking tired/out of breath*). Gunakan **Present Perfect Continuous (`have been carrying`)**. |
| **7** | **B** | Setelah modal `will`, kata bantu HARUS bentuk dasar **HAVE** (bukan *has*). Perbaikan: *will have destroyed*. |
| **8** | **C** | Kejadian lupa kunci rumah terjadi LEBIH DULU dibanding tidak bisa masuk rumah kemarin. Gunakan **Past Perfect (`had left`)**. |
| **9** | **B** | Sinyal waktu `Up to the present moment` adalah penanda khas **Present Perfect (`has not issued`)**. |
| **10** | **D** | Penanda waktu `By the end of this current decade` menuntut **Future Perfect (`will have become`)**. |
| **11** | **B** | Chef menyiapkan bahan TERLEBIH DULU sebelum tamu datang. Pola tepat: `Past Perfect + BEFORE + Simple Past`. |
| **12** | **C** | Pak Bambang menjabat 8 tahun sebelum pindah tahun 2024 (kejadian lampau berurutan). Gunakan **Past Perfect (`had served`)**. |
| **13** | **B** | Menanyakan durasi tinggal di Jakarta *sebelum* pindah ke Bali (dua masa lalu berurutan). Gunakan **Past Perfect Continuous (`had you been living`)**. |
| **14** | **B** | Penanda waktu `over the past few decades` menunjukkan aksi dari lalu hingga kini ➔ **Present Perfect (`have risen`)**. |
| **15** | **D** | Kombinasi tenggat waktu `By 10 PM tonight` + durasi `continuously for 12 hours` ➔ **Future Perfect Continuous (`will have been working`)**. |

### 🔑 Pembahasan & Kunci Jawaban Part II (Uraian)

**Jawaban Soal 1:**  
- **Alasan Salah:** Klausa setelah *by the time* yang merujuk masa depan TIDAK BOLEH menggunakan modal *will* (*will graduate*). Klausa tersebut harus berbentuk **Present Simple**.
- **Kalimat Perbaikan:** *"By the time I **graduate** from university next year, my parents **will have paid** off their mortgage."*

**Jawaban Soal 2:**  
- **Kalimat A (Simple Past):** Rudi pernah tinggal di Jogja selama 4 tahun di masa lalu, tapi **SEKARANG RUDI SUDAH TIDAK TINGGAL DI JOGJA LAGI** (kejadian sudah selesai total).
- **Kalimat B (Present Perfect):** Rudi mulai tinggal di Jogja 4 tahun lalu dan **SEKARANG RUDI MASIH TINGGAL DI JOGJA** (aksi berlanjut ke masa kini).

**Jawaban Soal 3:**  
- **Aturan yang Dilanggar:** Aturan **Stative Verbs Restriction**. Kata kerja *own* (kepemilikan) menggambarkan keadaan statis, bukan aktivitas aksi, sehingga dilarang menggunakan bentuk `-ing`.
- **Kalimat Perbaikan:** *"I **have owned** this car since 2020."*

**Jawaban Soal 4:**  
- **Urutan Kejadian:** 1. Kebakaran membakar toko (`Burned`) ➔ 2. Pemadam memadamkan (`Extinguished`) ➔ 3. Polisi menyelidiki (`Conducted`).
- **Kalimat Gabungan:** *"After the fire **had burned** three local shops, the fire department **extinguished** the fire before the police **conducted** an investigation."*

**Jawaban Soal 5 (Contoh Jawaban Ideal):**  
> *"By the time I **turn** twenty-five in 2030, I **will have earned** my bachelor's degree in Computer Science. Furthermore, I **will have launched** my own tech startup with my team. By then, we will have been developing software solutions for over two years."*

## 📊 Rubrik Penilaian Evaluasi

$$\text{Skor Total} = \text{Skor PG (15 Soal } \times 4) + \text{Skor Uraian (5 Soal } \times 8) = 60 + 40 = 100$$

- **Part I (Pilihan Ganda):** 4 Poin per nomor yang benar (Maksimal = 60 Poin).
- **Part II (Uraian HOTS):** Maksimal 8 Poin per nomor (Maksimal = 40 Poin):
  - **8 Poin:** Identifikasi salah tepat, analisis tata bahasa presisi, kalimat perbaikan 100% akurat.
  - **5 Poin:** Identifikasi tepat tapi perbaikan kalimat kurang sempurna.
  - **2 Poin:** Jawaban hanya benar sebagian kecil.
  - **0 Poin:** Salah total atau tidak menjawab.
