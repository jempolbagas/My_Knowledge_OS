### 📖 Reading Passage: *The Magic Fish and The Broken Promise*

> Once upon a time, in a quiet village near a dense forest, lived a poor fisherman named Danu. He lived a peaceful life, though he often struggled to find enough food.
>
> One rainy afternoon, Danu caught a small fish with glittering silver scales. To his surprise, the fish spoke with a soft voice, *"Please spare my life, kind fisherman! If you return me to the river, I will grant you a small pouch of gold coins."* Surprised yet compassionate, Danu gently released the fish back into the water. In an instant, a small golden pouch floated onto the riverbank.
>
> The fish gave Danu one strict condition, *"You may use this gold to feed your village, but you must never reveal to anyone where you obtained it. If you break your promise, the river will flood your entire village."* Danu promised faithfully and returned home.
>
> For months, Danu used the money to help his poor neighbors. However, his neighbor, Riko, grew suspicious of Danu's sudden wealth. Riko invited Danu for dinner and kept offering him sweet palm wine until Danu became intoxicated. In his careless state, Danu boasted and revealed everything about the magic fish and the river's promise.
>
> The next morning, dark clouds covered the sky. Continuous heavy rain poured, and the river began to overflow violently. Realizing his grave mistake, Danu ran to the riverbank and wept, begging for forgiveness. But the magic fish appeared only for a brief second and whispered, *"A promise broken cannot be unspoken."* The flood swept away Danu's house and fields, leaving him with nothing but a hard lesson on trust and integrity.

### 📌 Aktivitas 1: Generic Structure Mapping (Pemetaan Struktur Narasi)

Isilah tabel analisis *Generic Structure* berdasarkan cerita *The Magic Fish and The Broken Promise* di atas:

| Bagian Struktur Narasi | Paragraf ke- | Detail Peristiwa & Bukti Teks |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Orientation** | Paragraf 1 & 2 | **Tokoh:** Danu (fisherman), Magic Fish, Riko (neighbor)<br>**Latar Tempat & Waktu:** Quiet village near a forest, a rainy afternoon.<br>**Bukti Teks:** *"Once upon a time... lived a poor fisherman named Danu."* |
| **Complication** | Paragraf 3 & 4 | **Rising Action:** Danu menerima kantong emas dengan syarat tidak boleh membocorkan rahasia.<br>**Climax:** Danu mabuk dan ingkar janji membocorkan rahasia ikan ajaib kepada Riko. |
| **Resolution** | Paragraf 5 | **Akhir Cerita:** Banjir besar menerjang desa, rumah Danu hanyut, ikan ajaib menolak memaafkan.<br>**Tipe Ending:** *Sad Ending / Tragic Ending*. |
| **Re-orientation** | Paragraf 5 (implisit) | **Pesan Moral:** Menjaga amanah/janji sangat penting; sekali kepercayaan dirusak, penyesalan tidak dapat mengembalikan keadaan. |

### 📌 Aktivitas 2: Language Features Detector (Matriks Berburu Unsur Kebahasaan)

Temukan minimal 2 contoh dari masing-masing unsur kebahasaan dalam teks di atas:

```
[Simple Past Tense (V2)] ➔ Example: caught, spoke, released, promised, invited.
[Past Continuous Tense]  ➔ Example: (Drafted contextual sentence: "As he was walking...")
[Adverb / Connective of Time] ➔ Example: Once upon a time, One rainy afternoon, The next morning.
[Saying & Thinking Verbs] ➔ Example: spoke, whispered, boasted, begged, realized.
[Direct Speech]          ➔ Example: "Please spare my life, kind fisherman!"
```

### 📌 Aktivitas 3: Matriks Komparasi Karakter & Delik Moral (HOTS Case Study)

Bandingkan dilema moral tokoh dalam 3 cerita rakyat berikut melalui diskusi kelompok:

| Judul Cerita | Penyebab Utama Konflik (*Root Cause*) | Keputusan / Tindakan Tokoh | Dampak Akhir & Hukuman | Pelajaran Moral Utama (*Core Lesson*) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ***Malin Kundang*** | Kesombongan & malu mengakui ibu kandungnya yang miskin. | Membentak dan mengusir ibu kandungnya di depan istrinya. | Dikutuk menjadi batu di tepi pantai. | Hormatilah orang tua tanpa memandang harta dan status sosial. |
| ***Sangkuriang*** | Amarah tak terkendali & kebohongan membunuh Tumang. | Membunuh Tumang & memberi hatinya ke ibunya; memaksakan pernikahan. | Gagal memenuhi syarat, menendang perahu menjadi Gn. Tangkuban Perahu. | Kejujuran dan pengendalian emosi menentukan jalan hidup. |
| ***The Magic Fish*** | Kelalaian akibat terpengaruh minuman keras & pamer. | Menceritakan rahasia ikan ajaib kepada Riko saat mabuk. | Desanya diterjang banjir bandang dan kehilangan harta benda. | Komitmen terhadap janji/amanah adalah cermin integritas diri. |

**Questions 1 to 4 refer to the following text:**

> Once upon a time, in a green forest, a boastful Hare always mocked a slow Tortoise. Tired of the humiliation, the Tortoise challenged the Hare to a running race. The Hare accepted, laughing heartily. When the race started, the Hare ran like lightning while the Tortoise stepped slowly but steadily. Confident of winning, the Hare stopped under a tree and fell fast asleep. Meanwhile, the Tortoise kept moving without stopping until he crossed the finish line. When the Hare woke up, it was already too late.

1. **What was the primary reason the Tortoise challenged the Hare to a race?**  
   A. He wanted to show off his secret running skill.  
   B. He was fed up with the Hare's constant mocking and humiliation.  
   C. He wanted to claim the championship trophy of the forest.  
   D. The other animals forced him to race against the Hare.

2. **How did the Hare's personality lead to his defeat in the complication phase?**  
   A. His lack of physical endurance made him collapse.  
   B. His overconfidence made him underestimate his opponent and fall asleep.  
   C. He got lost in the dense forest while looking for food.  
   D. The Tortoise cheated by using a shorter secret path.

3. **What is the main idea of the resolution part of the story?**  
   A. The Hare woke up early and easily won the running race.  
   B. The Tortoise gave up midway because he was too exhausted.  
   C. The Tortoise won the race by maintaining a slow yet continuous effort.  
   D. The animals cancelled the race due to heavy rain.

4. **"The Hare accepted, *laughing heartily*." What does the underlined phrase reveal about the Hare's attitude?**  
   A. He was feeling nervous and tried to hide his fear.  
   B. He considered the challenge ridiculous and impossible for the Tortoise to win.  
   C. He was happy because the Tortoise was his best friend.  
   D. He was polite and appreciated the Tortoise's courage.

### ✍️ Section B: Soal Uraian Penalaran (5 Soal)

1. **Analisis Struktur (Structural Analysis):**  
   Jelaskan perbedaan mendasar antara bagian *Resolution* dengan *Re-orientation* dalam *Narrative Text*. Mengapa *Re-orientation* bersifat opsional?

2. **Analisis Karakter & Konflik (Character & Conflict Analysis):**  
   Dalam cerita *Cinderella*, terdapat dua jenis konflik yaitu konflik eksternal (dengan Ibu Tiri/Saudara Tiri) dan konflik internal (kesedihan dan harapan Cinderella). Jelaskan bagaimana ketabahan Cinderella membantu mengubah *Complication* menjadi *Happy Resolution*!

3. **Analisis Tata Bahasa (Grammar Analysis):**  
   Ubah kalimat dialog langsung (*Direct Speech*) berikut menjadi kalimat tidak langsung (*Indirect Speech / Reported Speech*):  
   > *Direct:* The prince said to Belle, "I will protect you from any danger in this castle."  
   > *Indirect:* __________________________________________________

4. **Penalaran Kritis (Critical Thinking):**  
   Banyak legenda Indonesia (seperti *Tangkuban Perahu* atau *Candi Prambanan*) diakhiri dengan kegagalan tokoh utama pria karena kecerdikan tokoh wanita (Dayang Sumbi / Roro Jonggrang). Menurut analisis penalaranmu, pesan nilai budaya apa yang ingin disampaikan oleh masyarakat pembuat cerita tersebut?

5. **Tugas Kreatif Penulisan (Creative Writing Task):**  
   Tuliskan 1 paragraf *Orientation* untuk cerita fabel buatanmu sendiri! Pastikan memuat: (a) Tokoh spesifik, (b) Latar waktu & tempat, (c) Minimal 1 *Adverb of Time*, dan (d) Minimal 2 *Adjectives*.

# PART III: KUNCI JAWABAN & PEMBAHASAN LENGKAP

## 🗝️ Kunci Jawaban Section A (Pilihan Ganda)

1. **B** — *Pembahasan:* Dalam teks disebutkan Tortoise merasa lelah dengan ejekan (humiliation) Hare sehingga ia memberanikan diri menantang Hare.
2. **B** — *Pembahasan:* Rasa percaya diri berlebihan (*overconfidence*) membuat Hare meremehkan lawan dan tertidur pulas di tengah lomba.
3. **C** — *Pembahasan:* Gagasan utama bagian resolusi adalah Tortoise memenangkan lomba berkat usahanya yang lambat namun konsisten (*slow but steady*).
4. **B** — *Pembahasan:* Ungkapan *laughing heartily* (tertawa terbahak-bahak) saat menerima tantangan menunjukkan sikap Hare yang menganggap tantangan kura-kura sangat konyol.
5. **C** — *Pembahasan:* Konflik utama cerita Aji Saka adalah kekejaman Prabu Dewata Cengkar yang gemar memakan daging manusia (teror terhadap rakyat).
6. **B** — *Pembahasan:* Aji Saka mengalahkan raja raksasa bukan dengan otot, melainkan dengan kecerdikan ikat kepalanya (*magic head cloth*) yang membentang luas menyorong raja ke laut.
7. **B** — *Pembahasan:* Pesan moral utama Aji Saka adalah kesewenang-wenangan dan kejahatan (*tyranny*) akan dikalahkan oleh kebijaksanaan dan keadilan.
8. **A** — *Pembahasan:* Kombinasi *Past Continuous* (`was sweeping`) untuk aksi yang sedang berlangsung dan *Simple Past* (`appeared`) untuk aksi menyela.
9. **B** — *Pembahasan:* Sesuai aturan *Direct Speech*, tanda koma dimasukkan di dalam petik penutup sebelum *saying verb* (`"...," whispered...`).
10. **C** — *Pembahasan:* Frasa tersebut memuat kumpulan kata sifat dan kata benda (*Noun Phrase*) untuk mendeskripsikan naga secara spesifik.

---

## 🗝️ Kunci Jawaban & Pembahasan Section B (Uraian)

1. **Jawaban:** *Resolution* adalah bagian wajib yang berisi penyelesaian konflik utama cerita (apakah berakhir bahagia, sedih, atau cliffhanger). Sedangkan *Re-orientation* berisi penutup, evaluasi, atau pesan moral dari penulis. *Re-orientation* bersifat opsional karena pesan moral sering kali sudah tersirat dalam *Resolution* tanpa harus dituliskan secara terang-terangan.
2. **Jawaban:** Ketabahan dan kebaikan hati Cinderella membuatnya tetap bersabar menghadapi perlakuan kejam ibu dan saudara tirinya. Kebaikan hatinya menarik bantuan Ibu Peri (*Fairy Godmother*) dan ketulusannya berdansa membuat Pangeran jatuh cinta. Sikapnya yang pemaaf di akhir cerita mengubah konflik penderitaan menjadi resolusi bahagia (*Happy Ending*).
3. **Jawaban:** *The prince told Belle that he would protect her from any danger in that castle.*
4. **Jawaban:** Cerita-cerita tersebut menyampaikan pesan budaya bahwa kecerdikan, harga diri, dan perlindungan diri (yang diwakili oleh tokoh wanita) mampu mengalahkan kehendak sewenang-wenangan, pemaksaan, maupun nafsu kekuasaan (yang diwakili oleh kekuatan fisik laki-laki).
5. **Rubrik Jawaban Kreatif (Contoh Jawaban):**  
   *"Once upon a time, in a vibrant tropical rainforest, lived a small, witty mouse deer named Kiki. Every morning, Kiki walked along the crystal-clear river to find sweet wild berries."*  
   *(Kriteria: Tokoh=Kiki; Latar=vibrant tropical rainforest/crystal-clear river; Adverb=Once upon a time; Adjectives=vibrant, small, witty, sweet).*
