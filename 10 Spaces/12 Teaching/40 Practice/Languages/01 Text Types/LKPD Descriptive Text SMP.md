```text
My Mechanical Gaming Keyboard

It is a tenkeyless black keyboard with RGB backlighting that makes my study desk look very futuristic.
```

### B. Soal Uraian Penalaran (5 Soal)

1. **Descriptive vs. Report Text Analysis:**  
   Jelaskan perbedaan mendasar antara *Descriptive Text* dan *Report Text* berdasarkan sifat objek dan tujuannya! Berikan masing-masing 1 contoh judul yang membedakan keduanya!

2. **Language Features Analysis (Action Verbs vs. To Be):**  
   Buatlah 2 kalimat deskripsi tentang **sekolahmu**:
   * Kalimat (a) menggunakan *To Be* (is/are) untuk menggambarkan keadaan fisik atau suasananya.
   * Kalimat (b) menggunakan *Action Verb* (Simple Present Tense) untuk menggambarkan salah satu aktivitas rutin siswa di sana.

3. **Urutan Kata Sifat (Adjective Order DOSASCOMP):**  
   Koreksi dan susun frasa benda berikut sesuai aturan *Adjective Order*:  
   * *"a (Japanese - new - white - cute - plastic) toy"*

4. **Deteksi Kesalahan Teks (Proofreading):**  
   Temukan 3 kesalahan tata bahasa (*grammar errors*) yang berkaitan dengan *Simple Present Tense/Verb agreement* dalam paragraf pendek berikut dan tuliskan perbaikannya:  
   > *"My grandfather is seventy years old, but he look very healthy. He like to walk around the garden every morning. His garden are full of colorful flowers."*

5. **Penulisan Deskriptif Singkat:**  
   Tuliskan 1 paragraf deskripsi (minimal 4 kalimat) tentang **hewan peliharaan favoritmu atau hewan impianmu**, sertakan minimal 3 kata sifat (*adjectives*), 1 *Adverb of Frequency*, dan 1 *Action Verb*!

### B. Pedoman Jawaban & Rubrik Soal Uraian

#### Kunci Jawaban Uraian:
1. **Perbedaan Descriptive vs. Report Text:**
   * **Descriptive Text:** Objek bersifat spesifik/khusus (cuma ada satu). Tujuannya menjelaskan detail unik objek tersebut. Contoh Judul: *My Fluffy Cat, Milo*.
   * **Report Text:** Objek bersifat umum/general (mewakili seluruh kelompok). Tujuannya menyajikan info ilmiah hasil observasi. Contoh Judul: *Cats*.
2. **Contoh Kalimat (Sekolah):**  
   * (a) *To Be:* "My school **is** spacious and very clean."  
   * (b) *Action Verb:* "Students **study** english in the library every Wednesday." (Action verb: *study*).
3. **Adjective Order (DOSASCOMP):**  
   * Urutan: Opinion (*cute*) $\rightarrow$ Age (*new*) $\rightarrow$ Color (*white*) $\rightarrow$ Origin (*Japanese*) $\rightarrow$ Material (*plastic*).  
   * **Hasil:** *"a cute new white Japanese plastic toy"*
4. **Proofreading (3 Kesalahan Grammar):**  
   * Kesalahan 1: *he look* $\rightarrow$ **he looks** (subjek *he* butuh verb+s).  
   * Kesalahan 2: *He like* $\rightarrow$ **He likes** (subjek *He* butuh verb+s).  
   * Kesalahan 3: *His garden are* $\rightarrow$ **His garden is** (subjek *garden* tunggal memakai *is*).
5. **Contoh Paragraf Deskriptif Pendek (Kucing):**  
   > *"I have a fluffy white Persian cat named Snow. He is very lazy because he **often** **sleeps** on my soft cotton pillow all day. Snow has bright blue eyes that sparkle beautifully. When he is hungry, he makes a gentle sound."*  
   > *(Adjectives: fluffy, white, lazy, soft, cotton, bright, blue, gentle | Adverb of Frequency: often | Action Verb: sleeps).*
