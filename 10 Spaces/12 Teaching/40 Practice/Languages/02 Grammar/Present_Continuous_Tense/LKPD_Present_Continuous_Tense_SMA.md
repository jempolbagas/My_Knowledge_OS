## 🔍 Activity 1: Guided Discovery & Text Mining (Menambang Wacana Progresif)

**Instruksi:** Bacalah wacana naratif tentang suasana ruang laboratorium inovasi teknologi SMA Garuda berikut dengan saksama:

> *"Inside the advanced AI and Robotics Laboratory, our development team **is currently assembling** a solar-powered disaster rescue drone. Look! Aditya **is soldering** the high-efficiency microchip onto the main motherboard, while Farhan **is running** real-time simulation benchmarks on his dual-screen workstation. We usually build ground-based rover robots, but this semester we **are developing** an aerial system for earthquake surveillance. Outside the window, a gentle afternoon drizzle **is falling**, yet everyone inside remains intensely focused. Tomorrow morning at 09.00, we **are meeting** the regional disaster management officials to present our live demonstration. However, Rian **is constantly forgetting** to back up his code repository, which causes slight delays in the workflow!"*

## ✍️ Activity 5: Authentic Micro-Writing Task (Tantangan Menulis Mikro)

**Instruksi:** Buatlah sebuah paragraf deskripsi situasi langsung (*live dynamic scene / lab work report*) bertema **"A Busy Hour at the Science Tech Fair"** (atau topik inovasi bebas pilihanmu) sepanjang **60–90 kata**.

**Kriteria Wajib:**
1. Menggunakan minimal **2 Kalimat Aksi Saat Ini (*Right Now*)** dengan *Attention Imperatives* (misal: *Look!* atau *Listen!*).
2. Menggunakan minimal **1 Kalimat Proyek Sementara (*Extended Present*)** menggunakan *these days* atau *this week*.
3. Menggunakan minimal **1 Kalimat Masa Depan Terjadwal (*Future Arrangement*)** dengan keterangan waktu spesifik.
4. Menggunakan minimal **1 Kalimat dengan *Stative Verb*** yang benar (menggunakan bentuk Simple Present).
5. Menggunakan minimal **1 Kalimat Ekspresi Kebiasaan Menjengkelkan (*Annoying Habit with 'Always'*)**.
