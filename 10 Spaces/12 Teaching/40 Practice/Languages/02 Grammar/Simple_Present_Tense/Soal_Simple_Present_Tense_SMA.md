## 🟢 Bagian I: Level 1 — Fondasi & Mekanika Dasar (Pilihan Ganda)

**Pilihlah satu jawaban yang paling tepat (A, B, C, atau D)!**

**1.** My uncle is a mechanic. He ______ various types of luxury cars in his workshop every day.
A. fix  
B. fixes  
C. fixing  
D. is fix  

**2.** The earth ______ on its axis once every 24 hours, which causes day and night.
A. rotates  
B. rotate  
C. is rotating  
D. does rotates  

**3.** Neither of the students ______ the answer to the teacher’s challenging question.
A. know  
B. knows  
C. is know  
D. are knowing  

**4.** Which of the following sentences is a **grammatically correct nominal sentence**?
A. He does very diligent in my class.  
B. My parents is in the living room.  
C. The new laptop is extremely fast and lightweight.  
D. She are a famous digital artist.  

**5.** "Why ______ your little sister cry every time she sees a thunder?"
A. do  
B. does  
C. is  
D. are  

**6.** Bagas and his teammates ______ soccer after school on Tuesdays.
A. plays  
B. are play  
C. play  
D. playing  

**7.** Choose the sentence with the **correct spelling** for the third-person singular verb:
A. The bird flys high in the sky.  
B. Mother worrys too much about my health.  
C. The scientist carries out several experiments.  
D. He plaies the guitar beautifully.  

**8.** "Excuse me, ______ you know where the nearest post office is?"
A. are  
B. is  
C. does  
D. do  

**9.** Jessica does not ______ coffee because it makes her heart beat faster.
A. drinks  
B. drink  
C. is drinking  
D. drank  

**10.** Which of the following sentences represents a **General Truth (Fakta Ilmiah)**?
A. I wake up at 6 AM every morning.  
B. Water freezes at zero degrees Celsius.  
C. The flight to Singapore departs at 8 PM.  
D. She is wearing a blue dress today.

## 🟡 Bagian II: Level 2 — Penerapan & Konteks (Pilihan Ganda)

**11.** Choose the sentence that places the **Adverb of Frequency** in the most natural and grammatically correct position:
A. She is late always for her morning lecture.  
B. She always is late for her morning lecture.  
C. She is always late for her morning lecture.  
D. Always she is late for her morning lecture.  

**12.** "Why are you holding your nose?" — "Because the garbage outside ______ terrible!"
A. smells  
B. is smelling  
C. smell  
D. are smelling  

**13.** The international conference ______ at 09:00 AM sharp next Monday according to the official schedule.
A. is going to start  
B. start  
C. starts  
D. starting  

**14.** Identify the sentence with a **Stative Verb** used incorrectly:
A. I believe you are telling the truth.  
B. We are possessing a huge collection of antique books.  
C. This smartphone belongs to my elder brother.  
D. Do you recognize the man standing near the gate?  

**15.** Complete the **Zero Conditional** sentence:  
*"If people ______ enough water, they ______ dehydrated very quickly."*
A. don't drink / get  
B. won't drink / get  
C. aren't drinking / are getting  
D. don't drink / will get  

**16.** Consider the verb *'think'*. In which sentence is *'think'* used as an **action verb in progress (Dynamic)**?
A. I think this project will be a huge success.  
B. What do you think about our new headmaster?  
C. She thinks he is the best candidate.  
D. Quiet, please! I am thinking about how to solve this math problem.  

**17.** In live sports broadcasting, the commentator shouts:  
*"Mbappé ______ past two defenders and ______ the ball into the top corner!"*
A. is running / is kicking  
B. runs / kicks  
C. ran / kicked  
D. has run / kicks  

**18.** Which of the following sentences is **grammatically flawed** due to double negation?
A. He never visits his hometown anymore.  
B. We seldom eat out during the weekdays.  
C. She doesn't rarely complain about overtime work.  
D. They hardly ever watch television.  

**19.** "I ______ for being late to the board meeting," said the executive. *(Performative Verb)*
A. am apologizing  
B. apologize  
C. was apologizing  
D. am apologize  

**20.** In formal inversion, *"He rarely speaks in public"* becomes:
A. Rarely he speaks in public.  
B. Rarely does he speak in public.  
C. Rarely does he speaks in public.  
D. Rarely is he speak in public.

## 🔴 Bagian III: Level 3 — HOTS Reasoning & Paragraph Editing (Uraian)

**21. (Contextual Verb Discrimination)**  
Jelaskan perbedaan nuansa makna gramatikal antara Kalimat A dan Kalimat B di bawah ini:
- **Kalimat A:** *“The chef tastes the curry.”*
- **Kalimat B:** *“The curry tastes delicious.”*

**22. (Zero Conditional Transformation)**  
Ubahlah premis ilmiah berikut menjadi 1 kalimat lengkap berstruktur **Zero Conditional** ($If / When$):  
*Premis: Logam dipanaskan $\to$ Logam memuai (expand).*

**23. (Formal Inversion Reconstruction)**  
Ubahlah kalimat standar berikut menjadi bentuk **Inversi Formal** menggunakan penekanan kata keterangan negatif di awal kalimat:  
*“They never suspect that their business partner is dishonest.”*

**24. (Pragmatic Register Analysis)**  
Mengapa pada tajuk rencana surat kabar (*newspaper headline*) seperti *"GOVERNMENT LAUNCHES NEW GREEN ENERGY POLICY"*, bentuk kata kerja yang digunakan adalah *Simple Present Tense*, padahal peluncuran kebijakan tersebut sebenarnya telah terjadi beberapa jam sebelum koran dicetak? Jelaskan fungsi pragmatisnya!

**25. (Master Paragraph Error-Correction Challenge)**  
Paragraf di bawah ini memuat **5 kesalahan gramatikal fatal**. Tuliskan kembali paragraf yang sudah diperbaiki secara utuh dan beri garis bawah (*underline*) pada setiap kata/frasa yang kamu perbaiki!

> *"Reza is a high school student who **love** computer science. He **is always wake up** at 5 AM because his school bus **is arriving** at 6:15 AM every day. He **does not has** a private motorcycle, so he relies on public transit. Right now, he **is wanting** to join a coding competition next month."*

## 🔑 Kunci Jawaban Lengkap & Pembahasan Analitis

### Pembahasan Bagian I (Level 1: Fondasi)

1. **Jawaban: B (fixes)**  
   *Pembahasan:* Subjek *My uncle* adalah orang ketiga tunggal (*He*). Kata kerja berakhiran desis `-x` wajib mendapat akhiran `-es` $\to$ *fixes*. Opsi D salah fatal karena mencampur *is* dan *verb 1*.
2. **Jawaban: A (rotates)**  
   *Pembahasan:* Fakta ilmiah mutlak (*General Truth*). Subjek *The earth* (tunggal $\to$ *It*), sehingga kata kerja *rotate* ditambah `-s` $\to$ *rotates*.
3. **Jawaban: B (knows)**  
   *Pembahasan:* Kata *Neither of + plural noun* secara gramatikal formal berstatus subjek tunggal (*singular subject*), sehingga memerlukan kata kerja bersuffix `-s` (*knows*).
4. **Jawaban: C (The new laptop is extremely fast and lightweight.)**  
   *Pembahasan:* Kalimat nominal menggunakan *To Be (is)* diikuti Adjective (*fast and lightweight*). Opsi A salah karena pakai *does* untuk adjective; Opsi B salah to be (*parents* $\to$ *are*); Opsi D salah to be (*she* $\to$ *is*).
5. **Jawaban: B (does)**  
   *Pembahasan:* Kalimat tanya verbal dengan subjek tunggal *your little sister* (kata ganti *she*) menggunakan auxiliary *does*.
6. **Jawaban: C (play)**  
   *Pembahasan:* Subjek *Bagas and his teammates* adalah subjek jamak (*They*), sehingga kata kerja kembali ke bentuk dasar (*base form*) tanpa `-s` $\to$ *play*.
7. **Jawaban: C (The scientist carries out several experiments.)**  
   *Pembahasan:* *carry* berakhiran konsonan `r + y`, maka `y` berubah menjadi `-ies` $\to$ *carries*. Opsi A harusnya *flies*, Opsi B harusnya *worries*, Opsi D harusnya *plays*.
8. **Jawaban: D (do)**  
   *Pembahasan:* *know* adalah kata kerja aksi/verbal. Kalimat tanya untuk subjek *you* menggunakan *do*.
9. **Jawaban: B (drink)**  
   *Pembahasan:* Setelah auxiliary negative *does not (doesn't)*, kata kerja utama wajib kembali ke bentuk asal (*base form*) tanpa `-s` $\to$ *drink*.
10. **Jawaban: B (Water freezes at zero degrees Celsius.)**  
    *Pembahasan:* Merupakan hukum fisika/fakta ilmiah abadi (*General Truth*).

### Pembahasan Bagian II (Level 2: Penerapan & Konteks)

11. **Jawaban: C (She is always late for her morning lecture.)**  
    *Pembahasan:* Aturan baku penempatan *Adverb of Frequency* pada kalimat nominal adalah **SETELAH To Be** (*Subject + To Be + Adverb*).
12. **Jawaban: A (smells)**  
    *Pembahasan:* *smell* di sini bermakna 'memiliki aroma tertentu' yang merupakan *Stative Verb of Perception/Sense*, sehingga tidak boleh dibuat continuous (*is smelling*).
13. **Jawaban: C (starts)**  
    *Pembahasan:* Jadwal resmi publik (*Timetabled Future Event*) menggunakan Simple Present Tense (*starts*).
14. **Jawaban: B (We are possessing a huge collection...)**  
    *Pembahasan:* *possess* (memiliki) adalah *Stative Verb of Possession* yang haram menggunakan continuous (*-ing*). Bentuk yang benar adalah: *We possess...*
15. **Jawaban: A (don't drink / get)**  
    *Pembahasan:* Rumus Zero Conditional untuk fakta sains/kondisi biologis mutlak: $If + \text{Simple Present}, \text{Simple Present}$.
16. **Jawaban: D (Quiet, please! I am thinking about how to solve this math problem.)**  
    *Pembahasan:* Pada opsi D, *think* bermakna aktivitas mental aktif yang sedang berlangsung (Dynamic Verb), sehingga sah menggunakan Present Continuous. Pada opsi A, B, C, *think* berarti 'berpendapat' (Stative).
17. **Jawaban: B (runs / kicks)**  
    *Pembahasan:* Komentator siaran langsung olahraga menggunakan Simple Present Tense untuk aksi instan beruntun di lapangan.
18. **Jawaban: C (She doesn't rarely complain about overtime work.)**  
    *Pembahasan:* Kata *rarely* sudah bermakna negatif, sehingga penggabungan dengan *doesn't* menciptakan kesalahan *Double Negative*. Bentuk yang benar: *She rarely complains...*
19. **Jawaban: B (apologize)**  
    *Pembahasan:* *Performative verbs* (kata kerja tindakan wicara) diucapkan dalam bentuk Simple Present: *I apologize*.
20. **Jawaban: B (Rarely does he speak in public.)**  
    *Pembahasan:* Inversi formal kalimat negatif: *Negative Adverb + Auxiliary (does) + Subject (he) + Base Verb (speak)*.

### Pembahasan Bagian III (Level 3: HOTS Reasoning)

21. **Perbedaan Makna *Tastes*:**
    - **Kalimat A (*The chef tastes the curry*):** Bersifat **Dynamic (Aksi Nyata)** di mana koki secara fisik sedang mencicipi/mengecap kuah kari menggunakan lidah/sendok.
    - **Kalimat B (*The curry tastes delicious*):** Bersifat **Stative (Karakteristik/Kondisi Indra)** di mana kari tersebut memiliki kualitas rasa lezat.

22. **Zero Conditional Transformation:**
    - **Jawaban:** *“If / When you **heat** metal, it **expands**.”* (atau *“Metal **expands** if / when you **heat** it.”*)

23. **Formal Inversion Reconstruction:**
    - **Jawaban:** *“__Never do they suspect__ that their business partner is dishonest.”*

24. **Analisis Fungsi Pragmatis Newspaper Headline:**
    - Simple Present pada judul berita digunakan untuk menciptakan efek kesegaran peristiwa (*dramatic immediacy*), menghemat karakter/ruang cetak, serta menarik perhatian pembaca seolah-olah peristiwa bersejarah tersebut baru saja terjadi di hadapan mereka saat ini.

25. **Perbaikan Paragraf Utuh:**
    > *"Reza is a high school student who **<u>loves</u>** computer science. He **<u>always wakes up</u>** at 5 AM because his school bus **<u>arrives</u>** at 6:15 AM every day. He **<u>does not have</u>** a private motorcycle, so he relies on public transit. Right now, he **<u>wants</u>** to join a coding competition next month."*
    
    *Daftar 5 Perbaikan:*
    1. *love* $\longrightarrow$ **loves** (Subjek *Reza / who* tunggal).
    2. *is always wake up* $\longrightarrow$ **always wakes up** (Hapus *is*, pasang adverb *always* sebelum verb 1 bertambah `-s`).
    3. *is arriving* $\longrightarrow$ **arrives** (Jadwal transportasi resmi publik $\to$ Simple Present).
    4. *does not has* $\longrightarrow$ **does not have** (Setelah kata bantu *does not*, kata kerja kembali ke *base form*).
    5. *is wanting* $\longrightarrow$ **wants** (*want* adalah Stative Verb yang tidak boleh berakhiran *-ing*).
