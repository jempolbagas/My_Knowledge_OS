## 🔍 Activity 1: Guided Discovery & Text Mining (Menambang Kalimat)

**Instruksi:** Bacalah teks narasi singkat tentang keseharian seorang Software Engineer muda bernama Sarah berikut ini.

> *"Sarah **is** a talented junior programmer at a tech startup in Jakarta. She **wakes up** at 5:30 AM every day and **drinks** a cup of warm water. She **does not drive** a car because the traffic in Jakarta **is** always chaotic; instead, she **takes** the MRT to her office. Her morning train **departs** at precisely 06:45 AM. At the office, she **collaborates** with her team, but she **prefers** working in silence when coding. In the evening, she **is** often tired, yet she **attends** an online English class twice a week. She **believes** that continuous learning **is** the key to a bright future."*

## ✍️ Activity 5: Authentic Creative Writing (Daily Habit & Fact Sheet)

**Instruksi:** Buatlah sebuah paragraf narasi pendek (6–8 kalimat) yang mendeskripsikan rutinitas belajarmu atau fakta unik tentang hobimu dalam bahasa Inggris!

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Checklist Syarat Tulisan Wajib:**
> - [ ] Memuat minimal **2 Kalimat Verbal Positif** (dengan subjek berbeda).
> - [ ] Memuat minimal **1 Kalimat Verbal Negatif** (*don't / doesn't*).
> - [ ] Memuat minimal **2 Kalimat Nominal** (menggunakan *is / am / are*).
> - [ ] Memuat minimal **2 Adverbs of Frequency** (*always, usually, sometimes, never*, dll.).
> - [ ] Memuat minimal **1 Stative Verb** (*love, prefer, believe, understand*, dll.).
