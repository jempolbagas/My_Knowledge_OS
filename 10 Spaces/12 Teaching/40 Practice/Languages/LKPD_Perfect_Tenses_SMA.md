## 🎯 Tujuan Pembelajaran
1. Peserta didik mampu mengidentifikasi penanda waktu (*time signals*) khas untuk 4 jenis Perfect Tenses secara akurat.
2. Peserta didik mampu mendeteksi kesalahan tata bahasa (*Error Identification*) pada format soal TOEFL Written Expression.
3. Peserta didik mampu melengkapi paragraf rumpang (*Cloze Test*) berstandar UTBK SNBT dengan tenses yang presisi.

---

## 📍 Bagian A: Time Signal & Formula Matching Drill

**Petunjuk:** Jodohkan klausa/penanda waktu di Kolom A dengan jenis Tense yang tepat di Kolom B!

| No | Kolom A (Klausa / Time Signal) | Kolom B (Tense yang Tepat) | Jawaban Kamu |
| :-: | :--- | :--- | :-: |
| 1 | *By the time the professor arrived...* | A. Present Perfect Tense | **1. .....** |
| 2 | *She has been living in Surabaya...* | B. Past Perfect Tense | **2. .....** |
| 3 | *By 2030, the company...* | C. Future Perfect Tense | **3. .....** |
| 4 | *...since 2019 up to now.* | D. Present Perfect Continuous | **4. .....** |
| 5 | *After they had completed the research...* | E. Simple Past Tense | **5. .....** |

## 📍 Bagian B: Error Spotting Drill (TOEFL Written Expression Style)

**Petunjuk:** Pilihlah satu kata atau frasa yang digarisbawahi (A, B, C, atau D) yang **SALAH / TIDAK TEPAT** secara tata bahasa!

1. By the time $\underset{\text{A}}{\text{the emergency team}}$ arrived at the village, the flood $\underset{\text{B}}{\text{has already destroyed}}$ several houses $\underset{\text{C}}{\text{and left}}$ many families $\underset{\text{D}}{\text{homeless}}$.
   - **Pilihan Salah:** ( ...... ) ➔ **Perbaikan:** ............................................................

2. Dr. Anita $\underset{\text{A}}{\text{will has published}}$ three scientific $\underset{\text{B}}{\text{articles}}$ in international journals $\underset{\text{C}}{\text{by the end of}}$ $\underset{\text{D}}{\text{this year}}$.
   - **Pilihan Salah:** ( ...... ) ➔ **Perbaikan:** ............................................................

3. The researchers $\underset{\text{A}}{\text{have been knowing}}$ each other $\underset{\text{B}}{\text{since}}$ they $\underset{\text{C}}{\text{were}}$ undergraduates at $\underset{\text{D}}{\text{Gadjah Mada University}}$.
   - **Pilihan Salah:** ( ...... ) ➔ **Perbaikan:** ............................................................

4. Before he $\underset{\text{A}}{\text{transferred}}$ to the Jakarta branch, $\text{Mr. Hendra}$ $\underset{\text{B}}{\text{worked}}$ for our company $\underset{\text{C}}{\text{for more than}}$ $\underset{\text{D}}{\text{a decade}}$.
   - **Pilihan Salah:** ( ...... ) ➔ **Perbaikan:** ............................................................

5. By next Friday, the contractors $\underset{\text{A}}{\text{will finish}}$ the construction of $\underset{\text{B}}{\text{the main tower}}$ $\underset{\text{C}}{\text{which started}}$ $\underset{\text{D}}{\text{last month}}$.
   - **Pilihan Salah:** ( ...... ) ➔ **Perbaikan:** ............................................................

## 📍 Bagian C: Contextual Cloze Test (UTBK Text Completion)

**Petunjuk:** Isilah titik-titik dalam paragraf berikut dengan bentuk kata kerja (*Verb*) dalam kurung yang paling tepat sesuai konteks tenses!

> **Text:**
> Artificial Intelligence (AI) technology $\text{1. \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ (transform)}$ global industries at an unprecedented rate over the past decade. Long before ChatGPT was launched in late 2022, computer scientists $\text{2. \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ (work)}$ on neural networks for several decades. By the time the current decade ends, AI integration $\text{3. \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ (become)}$ a standard requirement for almost every digital profession. Many tech analysts believe that companies which $\text{4. \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ (not adopt)}$ automation by 2030 will lag significantly behind their competitors.

**Lembar Jawaban Bagian C:**
1. Kata kerja `transform` ➔ Tense: .............................. ➔ Bentuk Tepat: ..............................
2. Kata kerja `work` ➔ Tense: .............................. ➔ Bentuk Tepat: ..............................
3. Kata kerja `become` ➔ Tense: .............................. ➔ Bentuk Tepat: ..............................
4. Kata kerja `not adopt` ➔ Tense: .............................. ➔ Bentuk Tepat: ..............................

## 📍 Bagian D: Sentence Transformation & Logic Challenge

**Petunjuk:** Gabungkan atau ubah dua kalimat terpisah menjadi satu kalimat padu menggunakan tenses yang diperintahkan!

1. **Kalimat 1:** The movie started at 7:00 PM.
   **Kalimat 2:** We arrived at the cinema at 7:15 PM.
   - ➔ **Instruksi:** Gabungkan menggunakan kata hubung **`By the time`** dan **Past Perfect Tense**!

2. **Kalimat 1:** I started studying English in 2020.
   **Kalimat 2:** It is now 2026, and I am still studying English.
