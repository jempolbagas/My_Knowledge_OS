# BAGIAN III: KUNCI JAWABAN, PEMBAHASAN, & RUBRIK PENILAIAN

## 🔑 Pembahasan Soal Pilihan Ganda

1. **Jawaban: B**
   - **Pembahasan:**
     Kecepatan maksimum tikungan datar: $v = \sqrt{\mu_s g R}$.
     Jika $\mu_s' = \frac{1}{4}\mu_s$, maka:
     $$v' = \sqrt{\frac{1}{4}\mu_s g R} = \frac{1}{2} \sqrt{\mu_s g R} = \frac{1}{2}v$$

2. **Jawaban: B**
   - **Pembahasan:**
     Tegangan tali ayunan konis: $T \cos\theta = mg \implies T = \frac{mg}{\cos\theta}$.
     $$T_1 = \frac{mg}{\cos 30^\circ} = \frac{mg}{\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{3}}$$
     $$T_2 = \frac{mg}{\cos 60^\circ} = \frac{mg}{\frac{1}{2}}$$
     $$\frac{T_2}{T_1} = \frac{\cos 30^\circ}{\cos 60^\circ} = \frac{\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{3}}{\frac{1}{2}} = \sqrt{3} : 1$$

3. **Jawaban: C**
   - **Pembahasan:**
     Pada puncak bukit cembung: $N = m(g - v^2/R)$.
     Sensasi melayang ($N = 0$):
     $$g - \frac{v^2}{R} = 0 \implies v = \sqrt{gR} = \sqrt{10 \times 40} = \sqrt{400} = 20 \text{ m/s}$$

4. **Jawaban: D**
   - **Pembahasan:**
     Kecepatan orbital satelit: $v = \sqrt{\frac{GM}{r}} \implies v \propto \frac{1}{\sqrt{r}}$.
     $$\frac{v_A}{v_B} = \sqrt{\frac{r_B}{r_A}} = \sqrt{\frac{4 r_A}{r_A}} = \sqrt{4} = 2 \implies 2 : 1$$

5. **Jawaban: B** (atau **D** jika dibulatkan desimal)
   - **Pembahasan:**
     $$v_{\max} = \sqrt{g R \left( \frac{\tan\theta + \mu_s}{1 - \mu_s \tan\theta} \right)}$$
     Untuk $\theta = 45^\circ \implies \tan 45^\circ = 1$, dan $\mu_s = 0.5$:
     $$v_{\max} = \sqrt{10 \times 90 \times \left( \frac{1 + 0.5}{1 - 0.5 \times 1} \right)} = \sqrt{900 \times \left( \frac{1.5}{0.5} \right)} = \sqrt{900 \times 3} = 30\sqrt{3} \text{ m/s} \approx 51.96 \text{ m/s}$$
     Pilihan paling tepat secara persis adalah **B** ($30\sqrt{3} \text{ m/s}$).

## 🔑 Pembahasan Soal Uraian

### 1. Pembahasan Uraian 1: Jangkauan Kecepatan Aman Tikungan Miring
- **Diketahui:** $\theta = 37^\circ \implies \tan 37^\circ = 0.75$, $R = 50 \text{ m}$, $\mu_s = 0.2$, $g = 10 \text{ m/s}^2$.
- **Langkah Perhitungan:**
  - **Kecepatan Maksimum ($v_{\max}$):**
    $$v_{\max} = \sqrt{g R \left( \frac{\tan\theta + \mu_s}{1 - \mu_s \tan\theta} \right)} = \sqrt{10 \times 50 \times \left( \frac{0.75 + 0.2}{1 - (0.2 \times 0.75)} \right)}$$
    $$v_{\max} = \sqrt{500 \times \left( \frac{0.95}{1 - 0.15} \right)} = \sqrt{500 \times \frac{0.95}{0.85}} = \sqrt{500 \times 1.1176} = \sqrt{558.8} \approx 23.64 \text{ m/s}$$
  - **Kecepatan Minimum ($v_{\min}$):**
    $$v_{\min} = \sqrt{g R \left( \frac{\tan\theta - \mu_s}{1 + \mu_s \tan\theta} \right)} = \sqrt{500 \times \left( \frac{0.75 - 0.2}{1 + (0.2 \times 0.75)} \right)}$$
    $$v_{\min} = \sqrt{500 \times \left( \frac{0.55}{1.15} \right)} = \sqrt{500 \times 0.4783} = \sqrt{239.1} \approx 15.46 \text{ m/s}$$
- **Kesimpulan:** Jangkauan kecepatan aman adalah $15.46 \text{ m/s} \le v \le 23.64 \text{ m/s}$.

---

### 2. Pembahasan Uraian 2: Ketinggian Minimum & Gaya Normal Roller Coaster
- **Pembuktian Bagian A:**
  - Di puncak loop ($y = 2R$), syarat kelajuan kritis $v_{\text{puncak}} = \sqrt{gR}$.
  - Hukum Kekekalan Energi Mekanik dari titik lepas $h$ ke puncak $2R$:
    $$mgh = mg(2R) + \frac{1}{2}m v_{\text{puncak}}^2$$
    $$gh = 2gR + \frac{1}{2}(gR) = 2.5 gR \implies h_{\min} = 2.5 R$$ *(Terbukti!)*

- **Perhitungan Bagian B:**
  - Di dasar lembah ($y = 0$), energi potensial berubah total jadi energi kinetik:
    $$mgh = \frac{1}{2} m v_{\text{dasar}}^2 \implies mg(2.5R) = \frac{1}{2} m v_{\text{dasar}}^2 \implies v_{\text{dasar}}^2 = 5 g R$$
  - Gaya normal di dasar lembah:
    $$N = m \left(g + \frac{v_{\text{dasar}}^2}{R}\right) = m \left(g + \frac{5gR}{R}\right) = m (6g) = 6 mg$$
    Untuk $m = 60 \text{ kg}$, $g = 10 \text{ m/s}^2$:
    $$N = 6 \times (60 \times 10) = 3.600 \text{ N}$$ (Sensasi $6G$).

---

### 3. Pembahasan Uraian 3: Ayunan Konis
- **Bagian A (Tegangan Tali):**
  $$T \cos 60^\circ = mg \implies T (0.5) = 0.5 \times 10 \implies T = 10 \text{ N}$$

- **Bagian B (Periode Putaran):**
  $$T_{\text{periode}} = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{L \cos\theta}{g}} = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{1.0 \times \cos 60^\circ}{10}} = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{0.5}{10}} = 2\pi \sqrt{0.05} \approx 2\pi (0.2236) \approx 1.40 \text{ s}$$

---

### 4. Pembahasan Uraian 4: Dinamika Lintasan Bergelombang
- **Diketahui:** $v = 15 \text{ m/s}$, $m = 1.000 \text{ kg}$, $R_1 = 50 \text{ m}$, $R_2 = 30 \text{ m}$.
- **a. Puncak Bukit Cembung:**
  $$N_1 = m \left( g - \frac{v^2}{R_1} \right) = 1000 \left( 10 - \frac{225}{50} \right) = 1000 (10 - 4.5) = 5.500 \text{ N}$$
- **b. Dasar Lembah Cekung:**
  $$N_2 = m \left( g + \frac{v^2}{R_2} \right) = 1000 \left( 10 + \frac{225}{30} \right) = 1000 (10 + 7.5) = 17.500 \text{ N}$$
- **c. Analisis Sensasi Fisis:**
  Di puncak bukit, pengemudi merasakan gaya normal berkurang ($5.500 \text{ N} < 10.000 \text{ N}$ Gaya Berat) sehingga terasa lebih "ringan / melayang". Di dasar lembah, gaya normal melonjak menjadi $17.500 \text{ N}$ ($1.75G$), menyebabkan penumpang merasa "tertekan kuat ke bawah".

---

### 5. Pembahasan Uraian 5: Orbit Satelit Geostasioner
- **a. Jari-Jari Orbit $r$:**
  $$T^2 = \frac{4\pi^2}{GM} r^3 \implies r^3 = \frac{G M T^2}{4\pi^2}$$
  $$r^3 = \frac{(6.67 \times 10^{-11}) \times (6.0 \times 10^{24}) \times (86400)^2}{4 \times (3.14159)^2}$$
  $$r^3 = \frac{4.002 \times 10^{14} \times 7.46496 \times 10^9}{39.4784} = \frac{2.9875 \times 10^{24}}{39.4784} \approx 7.567 \times 10^{22} \text{ m}^3$$
  $$r = \sqrt[3]{7.567 \times 10^{22}} \approx 4.23 \times 10^7 \text{ m} = 42.300 \text{ km}$$ (dari pusat bumi).

- **b. Kelajuan Orbital $v$:**
  $$v = \sqrt{\frac{GM}{r}} = \sqrt{\frac{6.67 \times 10^{-11} \times 6.0 \times 10^{24}}{4.23 \times 10^7}} = \sqrt{\frac{4.002 \times 10^{14}}{4.23 \times 10^7}} = \sqrt{9.461 \times 10^{6}} \approx 3.076 \text{ m/s} = 3.08 \text{ km/s}$$
