## ⚡ Master Formula & Rules Cheat Sheet

Simpan dan pahami tabel ringkasan rumus di bawah ini sebagai rujukan kilat saat belajar atau mengerjakan soal:

### 1. Matriks Struktur Kalimat (Verbal vs. Nominal)

| Jenis Kalimat | Kalimat Positif (+) | Kalimat Negatif (-) | Kalimat Tanya (?) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Verbal** *(Semua Subjek: I, You, We, They, He, She, It)* | **Subject** + **V₂** + **Object / Complement**<br>👉 *She **visited** Jogja last week.* | **Subject** + **did not (didn't)** + **V₁** + ...<br>👉 *She **didn't visit** Jogja last week.* | **Did** + **Subject** + **V₁** + ...?<br>👉 ***Did** she **visit** Jogja last week?* |
| **Nominal** *(I, He, She, It, Singular)* | **Subject** + **was** + **ANA**<br>👉 *He **was** tired yesterday.* | **Subject** + **was not (wasn't)** + **ANA**<br>👉 *He **wasn't** tired yesterday.* | **Was** + **Subject** + **ANA?**<br>👉 ***Was** he tired yesterday?* |
| **Nominal** *(You, We, They, Plural)* | **Subject** + **were** + **ANA**<br>👉 *They **were** happy at the party.* | **Subject** + **were not (weren't)** + **ANA**<br>👉 *They **weren't** happy at the party.* | **Were** + **Subject** + **ANA?**<br>👉 ***Were** they happy at the party?* |

> [!NOTE]
> **ANA** adalah singkatan dari **Adjective** (kata sifat: *happy, tired, sick*), **Noun** (kata benda: *a doctor, students*), atau **Adverb of Place/Time** (kata keterangan tempat/waktu: *at home, in Paris*).

### 2. Time Signals (Keterangan Waktu Khas Lampau)

Ciri utama Simple Past Tense adalah keberadaan titik waktu lampau yang **spesifik dan sudah berakhir**:

| Kategori Time Signal | Anggota Keterangan Waktu | Contoh Penggunaan Kalimat |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Keluarga AGO** | • *two hours ago*<br>• *three days ago*<br>• *a year ago*<br>• *a long time ago* | *The package arrived two hours ago.* |
| **Keluarga LAST** | • *last night*<br>• *last week*<br>• *last month / year*<br>• *last summer / Monday* | *We met the CEO last Monday.* |
| **Keluarga YESTERDAY** | • *yesterday*<br>• *yesterday morning*<br>• *yesterday afternoon*<br>• *the day before yesterday* | *She called me yesterday afternoon.* |
| **Titik Waktu Definit Lain** | • *in 1945 / in 2021*<br>• *just now*<br>• *this morning (jika sekarang sudah siang/malam)*<br>• *when I was a kid* | *Indonesia proclaimed its independence in 1945.* |

---

### 3. Golden Pronunciation Rules for "-ed" Suffix

Jangan lafalkan semua akhiran *-ed* sebagai "-ed" seperti Bahasa Indonesia! Gunakan 3 aturan fonetik ini:

| Kategori Bunyi Akhiran | Kapan Digunakan? | Contoh Kata Kerja ($V_1 \to V_2$) |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| 🔊 **/ɪd/** *(Ekstra Suku Kata)* | Setelah bunyi konsonan **/t/** atau **/d/** | • *want* $\to$ **wanted**<br>• *need* $\to$ **needed**<br>• *visit* $\to$ **visited** |
| 💨 **/t/** *(Voiceless - Tak Bersuara)* | Setelah konsonan tak bersuara: **/p/, /k/, /f/, /s/, /ʃ/ (sh), /tʃ/ (ch)/** | • *cook* $\to$ **cooked**<br>• *watch* $\to$ **watched**<br>• *laugh* $\to$ **laughed** |
| 🎶 **/d/** *(Voiced - Bersuara)* | Setelah konsonan bersuara: **/b/, /g/, /v/, /z/, /m/, /n/, /l/, /r/** & semua vokal | • *play* $\to$ **played**<br>• *clean* $\to$ **cleaned**<br>• *live* $\to$ **lived** |
