## 1. What is Simple Past Tense? (Hakikat & Garis Waktu Lampau)

**Simple Past Tense** digunakan untuk membicarakan suatu aksi, peristiwa, atau keadaan yang **dimulai dan telah SELESAI seutuhnya di masa lampau pada titik waktu yang definit (jelas)**.

> [!NOTE]
> **Visualisasi Garis Waktu (Timeline):**  
> **PAST (Masa Lalu)** $\longleftarrow$ **[X: Event Selesai]** $\longleftarrow$ ... ... $\longleftarrow$ **PRESENT (Saat Ini)**  
> *Peristiwa terjadi di titik X (misal: yesterday, 2024), selesai tuntas di titik X, dan tidak menyentuh masa kini.*

> [!KEYNOTE]
> **Karakteristik Mutlak Simple Past Tense:**
> 1. Peristiwa sudah berakhir 100% (*completed action*).
> 2. Tidak ada hubungan langsung dengan kondisi saat ini (*no present connection*).
> 3. Memiliki titik waktu lampau yang jelas (*definite time anchor*), baik tersurat (disebutkan langsung: *yesterday, two days ago*) maupun tersirat dari konteks narasi.

## 3. Kalimat Verbal (Verbal Sentences) 🏃‍♂️💨

**Kalimat Verbal** adalah kalimat di mana predikat utamanya adalah **Kata Kerja Aksi / Tindakan Nyata** (*Action Verb*), seperti *played, studied, visited, bought, went, saw*.

### 📐 Matriks Rumus Kalimat Verbal:

Kabar baiknya: Dalam kalimat positif Simple Past, **SEMUA SUBJEK memakai kata kerja bentuk yang sama ($V_2$)** tanpa perlu membedakan subjek tunggal (*he/she/it*) atau jamak (*they/we/I/you*)!

| Bentuk Kalimat | Pola Rumus Baku | Contoh Kalimat Nyata |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Positif (+)** | **Subject** + **V₂** + **Object / Adverb** | • *Budi **bought** a new mechanical keyboard yesterday.*<br>• *They **watched** the football derby last night.* |
| **Negatif (-)** | **Subject** + **did not (didn't)** + **V₁** + ... | • *Budi **didn't buy** a new mechanical keyboard yesterday.*<br>• *They **didn't watch** the football derby last night.* |
| **Tanya (?)** | **Did** + **Subject** + **V₁** + ...? | • ***Did** Budi **buy** a new mechanical keyboard yesterday?*<br>• ***Did** they **watch** the football derby last night?* |

---

### ⚠️ Hukum Netralisasi Auxiliary "Did" (The Golden Rule)

Perhatikan perubahan kata kerja pada kalimat negatif (-) dan interogatif (?) di atas!

> [!WARNING]
> **HUKUM NETRALISASI MUTLAK:**  
> > **DID / DIDN'T + VERB 1 (BASE FORM)**  
> 
> Kata bantu **`Did`** sebenarnya adalah bentuk lampau ($V_2$) dari kata kerja bantu *Do/Does*. Karena identitas lampau kalimat sudah diwakili secara sah oleh `Did`, maka kata kerja utama **WAJIB KEMBALI KE BENTUK DASAR ($V_1$)**:
> - ❌ *He didn't **came** to my birthday party.*
> - ✅ **He didn't come to my birthday party.**
> - ❌ *Did you **saw** the meteor shower?*
> - ✅ **Did you see the meteor shower?**

---

### ❓ Jawaban Singkat (Short Answers):
* *Did you submit the English assignment on time?*
  * $\to$ **Yes, I did.** / **No, I didn't.**
* *Did Sarah call you this morning?*
  * $\to$ **Yes, she did.** / **No, she didn't.**

---

### 🔍 Formulasi Pertanyaan 5W + 1H (Wh- Questions)

Dalam Simple Past, terdapat perbedaan mekanisme yang sangat krusial antara **Object Question** dan **Subject Question**:

#### A. Object Question (Menanyakan Objek / Keterangan):
Menggunakan kata bantu **`Did`** + **`Verb 1`**.
> **Wh-Word + did + Subject + V₁?**
- *Where **did** you **spend** your holiday?* $\longrightarrow$ *I spent my holiday in Bali.*
- *What **did** she **eat** for breakfast?* $\longrightarrow$ *She ate pancakes.*
- *Why **did** they **cancel** the tournament?* $\longrightarrow$ *Because of heavy rain.*

#### B. Subject Question (Menanyakan Subjek Pelaku):
Ketika kata tanya (*Who / What*) berfungsi sebagai **SUBJEK**, kita **TIDAK MENGGUNAKAN** kata bantu *did*, melainkan langsung diikuti **`Verb 2`**!
> **Who / What + V₂ + Object / Adverb?**
- *Who **called** you last night?* (Bukan: ❌ *Who did call you?*) $\longrightarrow$ *Dimas called me.*
- *What **happened** in the laboratory?* (Bukan: ❌ *What did happen?*) $\longrightarrow$ *An explosion happened.*
- *Who **broke** the antique vase?* $\longrightarrow$ *The cat broke it.*

## 4. Kalimat Nominal (Nominal Sentences / Stative Be) 🧘‍♂️✨

**Kalimat Nominal** adalah kalimat yang predikatnya **BUKAN kata kerja aksi**, melainkan menjelaskan keadaan, status, identitas, sifat, atau lokasi subjek di masa lampau menggunakan kata kerja bantu **`Was`** atau **`Were`**.

### 👥 Pembagian Pasangan Subjek & To Be Lampau:

| To Be Lampau | Pasangan Subjek | Contoh Subjek |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **WAS** | `I, He, She, It` & Subjek Tunggal (*Singular Nouns*) | *I, He, She, It, The teacher, Amanda, A cat* |
| **WERE** | `You, We, They` & Subjek Jamak (*Plural Nouns*) | *You, We, They, The students, Cats, Kevin and Rian* |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Perhatikan bahwa subjek **`I`** menggunakan **`was`** (bukan *were* seperti pada *are* di present tense, kecuali dalam kalimat pengandaian *Unreal Conditional*).

---

### 📐 Matriks Rumus Kalimat Nominal:

> **Pelengkap (Complement) = ANA (Adjective / Noun / Adverb)**

| Bentuk | Pola Rumus Baku | Contoh Kalimat Nyata |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Positif (+)** | **Subject** + **was / were** + **ANA** | • *Amanda **was** sick two days ago.* (Adjective)<br>• *They **were** active debate champions in 2023.* (Noun)<br>• *We **were** at the library yesterday afternoon.* (Adverb) |
| **Negatif (-)** | **Subject** + **wasn't / weren't** + **ANA** | • *Amanda **wasn't** sick two days ago.*<br>• *They **weren't** in the classroom when the bell rang.* |
| **Tanya (?)** | **Was / Were** + **Subject** + **ANA?** | • ***Was** Amanda sick two days ago?*<br>• ***Were** you at home last night?* |

---

### ❓ Jawaban Singkat & Wh- Questions Nominal:
* *Were you nervous during the presentation?* $\to$ **Yes, I was.** / **No, I wasn't.**
* *Was the exam difficult?* $\to$ **Yes, it was.** / **No, it wasn't.**
* *Where **were** you yesterday at 8 PM?* $\to$ *I **was** in my bedroom.*
* *Why **was** the teacher angry?* $\to$ *Because the students **were** noisy.*

## 5. Analisis Kesalahan Fatal & Jebakan Ujian (Pitfalls & Antidotes)

Berikut adalah 4 kekeliruan fatal yang paling sering muncul dalam ujian dan cara menghindarinya:

| Kekeliruan Fatal | Kenapa Salah? | Perbaikan yang Tepat (Antidote) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ❌ *I **was go** to Malang.* | Menggabungkan *To Be* dengan kata kerja aksi dasar secara ilegal. | ✅ **I went to Malang.** (Verbal) |
| ❌ *Did they **arrived** safely?* | Melanggar hukum netralisasi *Did* ($Did + V_2$ adalah haram). | ✅ **Did they arrive safely?** ($Did + V_1$) |
| ❌ *She **didn't was** happy.* | Memakai *didn't* untuk menyangkal kata sifat tanpa kata kerja aksi. | ✅ **She wasn't happy.** (Nominal) |
| ❌ *We **was** very excited.* | Ketidaksesuaian subjek jamak (*We*) dengan to be singular (*was*). | ✅ **We were very excited.** |

Ujilah pemahamanmu dengan memperbaiki kalimat berikut dalam 2 menit:

1. *Rafi didn't bought the ticket yesterday.* $\to$ **Revisi:** `___________________________________`
2. *Were they finish the group project last night?* $\to$ **Revisi:** `___________________________________`
3. *Who did discover this ancient artifact?* $\to$ **Revisi:** `___________________________________`
4. *She was very busy when I called her.* $\to$ **Status:** `[Benar / Salah]`
5. *Why did you was late to the seminar?* $\to$ **Revisi:** `___________________________________`

---
## Kunci Jawaban Mini Checkpoint

1. **Rafi didn't buy the ticket yesterday.** (Auxiliary *didn't* meleburkan *bought* kembali ke $V_1$ *buy*).
2. **Did they finish the group project last night?** (Kalimat verbal aksi *finish* memerlukan kata bantu *Did*, bukan *Were*).
3. **Who discovered this ancient artifact?** (Subject Question: menanyakan pelaku subjek langsung memakai $V_2$ tanpa *did*).
4. **Benar** (Kalimat nominal lampau yang tepat: *She* + *was* + Adjective *busy*).
5. **Why were you late to the seminar?** (Kalimat nominal tanya: *Wh-word* + *were* + Subject *you* + Adjective *late*).
