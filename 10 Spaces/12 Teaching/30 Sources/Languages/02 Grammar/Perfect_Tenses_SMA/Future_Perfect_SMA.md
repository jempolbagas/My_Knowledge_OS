# 🔮 Modul 03: Future Perfect Tense — Proyeksi Tenggat Waktu Masa Depan!

> **Imagine Your Graduation Day!** 🎓
> Sekarang kamu kelas 12 SMA (misal tahun 2026). Kamu buat janji sama diri sendiri: *"Bulan Juli tahun depan (2027), aku **sudah lulus** SMA dan **sudah diterima** di PTN impian!"*.
> 
> Perhatikan frasa **"SUDAH LULUS SEBELUM BULAN JULI TAHUN DEPAN"**. Kamu memproyeksikan sebuah titik di masa depan (Juli 2027), dan memastikan sebuah aksi **sudah kelar 100% sebelum titik tersebut**. Inilah fungsi utama **Future Perfect Tense**!

---

## 1. Core Concept: "Target & Deadline in the Future" 🎯

Future Perfect Tense digunakan untuk menyatakan aksi atau keadaan yang **diperkirakan/diproyeksikan akan selesai SEBELUM tenggat waktu (deadline) tertentu di masa depan**.

> [!KEYNOTE]
> **The Golden Formula Concept:**
> $$\text{Future Point (Deadline)} \leftarrow \text{ACTION COMPLETED BEFORE THAT POINT}$$
> Syarat mutlak Future Perfect Tense: Harus ada **Keterangan Waktu Batas (Deadline Marker)** di masa depan! Jika tidak ada batas waktu, gunakan Future Simple biasa.

---

## 2. Duo Formulas: Verbal vs. Nominal 📐

Sama seperti Past Perfect, kata bantu **`WILL HAVE`** digunakan untuk **SEMUA SUBJEK** (*I, You, We, They, He, She, It*).

### A. Verbal Sentences (Kalimat Verbal) 🏃‍♂️

| Bentuk Kalimat | Formula / Rumus | Contoh Kalimat |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Positif (+)** | `Subject + WILL HAVE + Verb 3 + Complement` | *By 2030, scientists **will have developed** a new cure.* |
| **Negatif (-)** | `Subject + WILL NOT (WON'T) HAVE + Verb 3` | *They **will not have completed** the bridge by December.* |
| **Tanya (?)** | `WILL + Subject + HAVE + Verb 3 + Complement?` | * **Will** you **have finished** your report by 5 PM?* |

---

### B. Nominal Sentences (Kalimat Nominal) 🧑‍🏫

| Bentuk Kalimat | Formula / Rumus | Contoh Kalimat |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Positif (+)** | `Subject + WILL HAVE + BEEN + Complement` | *By next year, I **will have been** in this city for 5 years.* |
| **Negatif (-)** | `Subject + WILL NOT (WON'T) HAVE + BEEN` | *She **won't have been** ready by the time the bus arrives.* |
| **Tanya (?)** | `WILL + Subject + HAVE + BEEN + Complement?` | * **Will** he **have been** a CEO by the age of thirty?* |

---

## 3. Mandatory Time Markers (Penanda Batas Waktu) ⏰

Soal UTBK & TOEFL sangat mudah dikenali karena hampir SELALU mengandung pola penanda waktu berikut:

### 1. `BY` + Future Time Expression
- *By tomorrow morning...*
- *By next Friday...*
- *By the end of this semester...*
- *By the year 2028...*
- *Example:* *By the end of this month, we **will have saved** \$1,000.*

### 2. `BY THE TIME` + Clause in Present Simple (`V1 / V-s/es`)
$$\text{Future Perfect (will have + V3)} + \text{BY THE TIME} + \text{Present Simple (V1)}$$
- *By the time you **wake up** tomorrow, I **will have arrived** in Tokyo.*
- *By the time the professor **starts** the lecture, the students **will have submitted** their papers.*

> [!CAUTION]
> **Hati-Hati Klausa "By the time":**
> Kata kerja setelah *by the time* HARUS dalam bentuk **Present Simple (`V1`)**, BUKAN Future Simple (`will`)!
> - ❌ *By the time you **will arrive**, I will have left.* (SALAH BANYAK!)
> - ✅ *By the time you **arrive**, I **will have left**.* (BENAR BETH!)

---

## 4. Comparison Trio: Future Simple vs. Future Continuous vs. Future Perfect ⚔️

Mari lihat bedanya tiga tenses masa depan ini pada jam yang sama (misal besok jam 08.00 pagi):

```
┌───────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Tense                 │ Formula                       │ Makna & Maksud Kalimat                    │
├───────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Future Simple         │ I will work at 8 AM.          │ Saya AKAN MULAI bekerja tepat jam 8.      │
│ Future Continuous     │ I will be working at 8 AM.    │ Saya SEDANG BANYAK KERJAAN jam 8 (lagi    │
│                       │                               │ berlangsung).                             │
│ Future Perfect        │ I will have worked by 8 AM.   │ Pekerjaan saya SUDAH KELAR SEMUA sebelum  │
│                       │                               │ jam 8.                                    │
└───────────────────────┴───────────────────────────────┴───────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## ⚠️ UTBK & TOEFL Error Identification Traps!

> [!WARNING]
> **Jebakan 1: Lupa Menggunakan "HAVE" Setelah "WILL" (Tertukar Has)**
> Meskipun subjeknya *He, She, It*, modal *will* HARUS diikuti oleh kata kerja bentuk dasar (*bare infinitive*), yaitu **HAVE** (bukan *has*!).
> - ❌ *She **will has** finished by 3 PM.* (SALAH FATAL!).
> - ✅ *She **will have** finished by 3 PM.*
