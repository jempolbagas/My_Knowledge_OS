## 15 Contoh Narrative Text Beserta Strukturnya | Bahasa Inggris Kelas 8

![contoh narrative text](https://cdn-web-2.ruangguru.com/landing-pages/assets/5a1ceeb4-a35f-4344-96a6-e36f068c0247.png)

> *Belajar narrative text dalam bahasa Inggris, yuk! Simak contoh narrative text berikut ini dan pelajari struktur teks maupun kalimatnya.*

—

Pernah nggak sih kamu membaca cerita sebelum tidur, entah dongeng tentang putri dan pangeran, kisah kancil yang cerdik, atau legenda rakyat seperti Malin Kundang? Cerita-cerita itu bukan sekadar hiburan, tapi juga mengandung pesan moral yang bisa kita pelajari. Nah, dalam bahasa Inggris, cerita semacam itu disebut *narrative text*.

*Narrative text* adalah teks yang bertujuan untuk menceritakan suatu kejadian dengan cara menarik sekaligus menghibur. Jenis-jenis narrative text, di antaranya legenda, dongeng, fabel, hingga mitos. Bahkan, *narrative text* juga bisa ditulis berdasarkan kisah nyata di kehidupan sehari-hari kita, loh.

Nah, karena sebelumnya kita sudah membahas cukup banyak tentang pengertian, *generic structure*, hingga *language features* dari *narrative text*, sekarang saatnya kita masuk ke bagian yang paling seru, yaitu membuat contoh *narrative text*.

**Baca Juga: [Pengertian Narrative Text, Struktur, Jenis & Contoh](https://www.ruangguru.com/blog/struktur-dari-narrative-text)**

Tapi, jangan buru-buru dulu. Dalam menulis *narrative text*, kita tidak bisa asal menuangkan cerita begitu saja. Supaya kisah yang kita tulis benar-benar “ *ngena* ” ke pembaca, cerita harus disusun dengan alur yang jelas. Itulah sebabnya, kita perlu memahami *generic structure* dari *narrative text*. Struktur inilah yang menjadi kerangka dasar, agar cerita lebih mengalir, mudah dipahami, sekaligus memberikan kesan mendalam pada pembaca.

Secara umum, *generic structure* dalam *narrative text* terdiri dari:

- *Orientation,* bagian pembuka yang **memperkenalkan tokoh, latar waktu, dan tempat**.
- *Complication,* bagian inti cerita yang **memunculkan konflik atau masalah**.
- *Resolution,* bagian penyelesaian konflik, bisa berupa **solusi akhir** yang bahagia, atau justru akhir yang menyedihkan.
- *Re-orientation* (bersifat opsional), bagian penutup yang berisi **pesan moral, amanat, atau refleksi** dari cerita.

Untuk lebih jelasnya, yuk kita perhatikan beberapa contoh *narrative text* pendek dalam berbagai tema. Dari contoh-contoh ini, kamu bisa melihat bagaimana setiap bagian struktur *narrative text* diterapkan dengan baik.

## 1\. Contoh Narrative Text Legend

***Sangkuriang and Dayang Sumbi***

***Orientation:***

*Once upon a time, in the land of Priangan, lived a family with a unique story. The father was not an ordinary man but a dog named Tumang, who was actually a cursed god. The mother, a beautiful and kind woman, was called Dayang Sumbi, and together they had a son named Sangkuriang.*

*One day, Dayang Sumbi asked her son to go hunting in the forest, accompanied by his faithful dog, Tumang. They spent the whole day searching for deer, but not even a single one appeared. Growing frustrated and desperate, Sangkuriang began to lose patience. In his anger, he made a terrible decision. He killed his own dog, Tumang, and took its liver home, pretending it was deer liver.*

***Complication:***

*At home, Dayang Sumbi prepared to cook the meat. But soon, she discovered the truth. The liver did not belong to a deer but to Tumang, her loyal companion. Realizing what Sangkuriang had done, Dayang Sumbi was furious. In her rage, she struck Sangkuriang’s head with a rice scoop, leaving a deep wound. The boy, hurt both physically and emotionally. He left home in anger and sorrow.*

*Years passed, Sangkuriang grew into a strong and handsome young man. He wandered across kingdoms, traveling to many places, until one day, he returned unknowingly to his homeland. In a village, he met a beautiful woman. They quickly fell in love and decided to marry.*

*However, as they discussed their wedding plans, Dayang Sumbi, who was still youthful and beautiful because of her divine powers, noticed the scar on the young man’s forehead. It looked exactly like the wound she had once inflicted on her son. Shocked and horrified, she realized that the man she was about to marry was none other than her own son, Sangkuriang.*

***Resolution:***

*Dayang Sumbi knew she could not marry Sangkuriang, but she also could not directly reveal the truth. To delay the marriage, she gave Sangkuriang an impossible task. He had to build a great lake and a huge boat in just one night, before the sun rose.*

*Driven by love and determination, Sangkuriang used his supernatural powers. He summoned spirits and worked tirelessly. By midnight, the lake was almost finished, and the giant boat was nearly complete. Dayang Sumbi grew anxious, fearing that Sangkuriang would succeed.*

*In desperation, she prayed for divine help. Then, she spread pieces of silk cloth across the eastern horizon and created the illusion of dawn. Roosters began to crow, thinking morning had come. Seeing this, Sangkuriang thought he had failed.*

*Furious and heartbroken, he kicked the giant boat with all his strength. The unfinished boat toppled over, landing upside down, and magically turned into a mountain. To this day, that mountain is known as Tangkuban Perahu, which means “upturned boat,” located in Bandung, West Java.*

***Re-orientation:***

*The story of Sangkuriang and Dayang Sumbi teaches us that lies and disobedience can lead to great tragedy. It also reminds us of the importance of patience and respecting those who care for us. In the end, greed and anger only bring destruction, while wisdom and sincerity bring peace.*

## 2\. Contoh Narrative Text Fable

***The Tortoise and The Hare***

***Orientation:***

*Once upon a time, in a lush green forest, there lived a hare and a tortoise. The hare was famous for his incredible speed. He often bragged to the other animals, saying, “No one can ever beat me in a race!” Whenever he ran, the dust flew behind him, leaving the others amazed.*

*On the other hand, the tortoise was known for being very slow. Every step he took was small and steady, and it always took him a long time to get anywhere. But unlike the hare, the tortoise was calm, patient, and never gave up, even though he was often laughed at by the boastful hare.*

***Complication:***

*One day, the hare challenged the tortoise to a race. The animals in the forest laughed loudly. “How could the tortoise possibly win against the hare?” they thought.* *Surprisingly, the tortoise accepted the challenge with confidence. “Alright, let’s prove it,” he said calmly.*

*The race began. The hare dashed off with lightning speed, leaving the tortoise far behind. Every now and then, the hare turned his head and teased, “Hey, tortoise! You’re still crawling back there?”*

*Feeling overconfident, the hare decided to stop under a big shady tree. “The tortoise is so slow. I might as well take a nap,” he thought. Soon, he fell into a deep sleep.*

*Meanwhile, the tortoise kept moving forward, slow but steady, never stopping. Step by step, he finally approached the finish line. By the time the hare woke up and ran as fast as he could, it was already too late. The tortoise crossed the finish line first, and all the animals cheered in surprise.*

***Resolution:***

*The hare was deeply embarrassed. He could not believe he had been defeated by such a slow creature. Determined to prove himself, he challenged the tortoise to another race. This time, he promised not to repeat his mistake.*

*From the very start, the hare ran at full speed and did not stop once. However, the tortoise had also learned something. Instead of using the same path, he chose a shorter, straighter trail and kept moving with full concentration. By the time the hare reached the finish line, the tortoise was already waiting there.*

*The hare was shocked. He realized that speed alone was not enough. But, strategy, patience, and determination were just as important.*

***Re-orientation:***

*From that day on, the hare no longer mocked the tortoise. Instead, they became good friends. The hare learned to respect the tortoise’s perseverance, while the tortoise appreciated the hare’s speed. They often raced together, not to prove who was better, but to remind each other that in life, it is not always the fastest who wins, but the one who never gives up and runs the race wisely.*

## 3\. Contoh Narrative Text Fairy Tale

***Beauty and The Beast***

***Orientation:***

*Once upon a time, in a far-off land, there lived a wealthy merchant with three daughters. The youngest daughter was named Belle. Unlike her sisters, who were often vain and selfish, Belle was gentle, kind-hearted, and loved reading books more than anything. Her beauty was admired by many, but it was her pure heart that made her truly special.*

***Complication:***

*One day, tragedy struck. The merchant lost all his fortune in a great storm at sea, leaving him and his daughters with nothing. They were forced to leave their luxurious mansion and move into a small, humble cottage deep in the forest.*

*Trying to rebuild his life, the merchant decided to travel to the city to sell some of his remaining goods. Before leaving, he asked his daughters what gifts they wanted him to bring back. The older sisters demanded fine dresses and jewels, but Belle simply requested a single rose.*

*On his way home, the merchant became lost in the dark forest. After wandering for hours in the cold, he stumbled upon an enchanted castle. Desperate and tired, he entered the castle, where he found food and a warm bed prepared for him, though no one was in sight. The next morning, as he was about to leave, he noticed a beautiful rose garden. Remembering Belle’s wish, he plucked a single rose.*

*Suddenly, a terrifying Beast appeared and roared furiously, accusing him of theft. Trembling, the merchant begged for forgiveness, explaining that the rose was for his beloved daughter. The Beast finally agreed to spare his life, but only on one condition, Belle must come to live in the castle forever.*

*When the merchant returned home, he sorrowfully told his daughters what had happened. Belle, seeing her father’s distress, bravely offered to take his place. At the castle, she was frightened at first, but the Beast treated her with unexpected kindness. Slowly, Belle realized that behind the Beast’s frightening appearance was a gentle and lonely soul. Day by day, their friendship grew, and eventually Belle’s feelings turned into love. The Beast, too, longed to marry her, but he feared that his monstrous form would stand in the way of their happiness.*

***Resolution:***

*One day, Belle asked the Beast if she could visit her father, who had fallen ill. Though saddened, the Beast agreed and gave her a magic mirror that allowed her to see him whenever she wished. Belle promised to return, but when she saw her father’s fragile condition, she stayed longer than she intended.*

*Heartbroken and believing that Belle had abandoned him, the Beast became gravely ill. When Belle saw in the mirror how weak he had become, her heart ached. She realized that she truly loved him. Without wasting time, she rushed back to the castle.*

*As she found the Beast lying helpless in the garden, Belle wept and confessed her love for him. Her sincere words broke the powerful curse. In an instant, the Beast transformed into a handsome prince.*

***Re-orientation:***

*The prince explained that only true love could lift the spell, and Belle’s love had freed him. Overjoyed, Belle and the prince were married in a grand celebration. They lived happily ever after in the castle, with Belle’s kindness and courage remembered by all as the true magic that turned a beast into a man.*

## 4\. Contoh Narrative Text Myth

**Keong Emas**

***Orientation:***

*Once upon a time, there lived a wise and noble king in a Javanese kingdom. Although his reign was prosperous and his people lived in harmony, the king had one deep sorrow, he had no heir to continue his legacy. Every day, he prayed to the gods and sought guidance from wise men.*

*One day, a mysterious old man appeared before him carrying a small golden snail. The old man told the king, “Take care of this snail, and your prayers will be answered.” The king, though confused, accepted the gift. To his surprise, the snail transformed into a beautiful young woman named Dewi Galuh. The king was captivated by her beauty and kindness, and eventually, they were married. Their love was blessed with the birth of a handsome son, Raden Putra.*

***Complication:***

*Unbeknownst to the king, Dewi Galuh was actually a celestial being who had been cursed into the form of a snail. The curse had one condition, if she ever laid her eyes upon a golden object, she would instantly return to her original snail form.*

*For many years, Dewi Galuh managed to live happily in the palace without encountering anything golden. But one fateful day, as she strolled through the royal garden, her gaze accidentally fell upon a rare golden flower that had just bloomed. In an instant, her body shrank and twisted back into the small golden snail.*

*A swallow that was flying nearby swooped down and swallowed the snail. When Raden Putra learned of his mother’s disappearance, he was devastated. Rumors spread through the palace that his mother had vanished without a trace. Determined to save her, Raden Putra set out on a long journey, following clues and even hunting down the swallow that had taken her away.*

***Resolution:***

*After days of searching, Raden Putra finally found the swallow and carefully retrieved the golden snail from its belly. With love and hope, he held the snail close and prayed to the gods to release his mother from the curse. Miraculously, the curse was broken. The snail shimmered with light and transformed once again into Dewi Galuh, more radiant than ever before.*

*The king rejoiced when he saw his wife and son reunited. The palace was filled with celebration, and the people praised the strength of Raden Putra’s love and devotion.*

***Re-orientation:***

*The tale of Keong Emas teaches us that true love, faith, and perseverance can overcome even the most powerful curses. It also reminds us about the importance of trust, patience, and courage in the face of hardship. For generations, this story has been passed down in Javanese culture, not only as entertainment but also as a moral lesson about family, love, and sacrifice. Today, Keong Emas remains one of Indonesia’s most beloved folklore stories.*

## 5\. Contoh Narrative Text Folktale atau Folklore

**Bawang Merah Bawang Putih**

***Orientation:***

*Once upon a time, in a peaceful small village in Indonesia, there lived a beautifull girl named Bawang Putih. She lived with her father. Unfortunately, her mother had passed away when she was still little. Her father eventually remarried a woman who had a daughter of her own, Bawang Merah.*

*At first, Bawang Putih hoped that her new stepmother and stepsister would treat her like family. However, as time passed, she realized that her stepmother was cruel and unfair. She always spoiled Bawang Merah and gave her everything she wanted, while Bawang Putih had to do all the household chores. Despite this, Bawang Putih remained patient, kind, and hardworking.*

***Complication:***

*One day, the stepmother ordered Bawang Putih to go to the river and wash a big pile of clothes. Obediently, she carried the heavy basket and went down to the river. While washing, Bawang Putih felt very tired and sad. She began to cry softly, her tears falling into the water.*

*Suddenly, a magic fish appeared from the river. The fish spoke gently and asked why she was crying. Surprised but comforted, Bawang Putih told the fish everything, how her stepmother always treated her badly, how her sister mocked her, and how lonely she felt.*

*Hearing her story, the magic fish felt pity and decided to help. It gave Bawang Putih a magical flower that glowed brightly. The fish told her, “Keep this flower safe. As long as you protect it, happiness will come to you.”*

*When Bawang Putih returned home, she showed the flower to her stepsister, Bawang Merah. Instead of being happy for her, Bawang Merah’s heart was filled with jealousy. She wanted the flower for herself. That night, while Bawang Putih was asleep, Bawang Merah secretly stole the magical flower.*

*The next morning, Bawang Merah went to the river pretending to wash clothes. She hoped the magic fish would appear again and give her something even better. Indeed, the fish appeared, but instead of offering her a gift, it scolded her harshly. The fish said, “You are selfish and wicked. You do not deserve any blessing, only punishment.”*

***Resolution:***

*Right after the fish spoke, the magical flower that Bawang Merah had stolen suddenly turned into a hard stone that stuck tightly to her hand. No matter how hard she tried, she could not remove it. From then on, people called her “Bawang Merah Berbatu” or “Onion with a Stone.”*

*Meanwhile, Bawang Putih, who always remained kind and patient, continued to live her life with a sincere heart. Her beauty and kindness spread across the village.*

*One day, a kind and wealthy prince heard about her. The prince met her, fell in love, and eventually married her. Bawang Putih finally lived a happy life filled with love and respect.*

***Re-orientation:***

*The story of Bawang Merah and Bawang Putih teaches us an important lesson, kindness, honesty, and compassion will always lead to happiness, while greed, envy, and cruelty will only bring misery. It reminds us that patience and a sincere heart are far more valuable than jealousy and selfishness.*

## 6\. Contoh Narrative Text Cinderella

**Cinderella**

***Orientation:***

*Long ago, in a peaceful village, there lived a happy family consisting of a father, a mother, and their beautiful daughter named Cinderella. She grew up in a loving environment where her parents cared for her deeply.*

*Unfortunately, happiness did not last long. When Cinderella was still very young, her beloved mother passed away. Her father, not wanting his daughter to grow up without a mother figure, later remarried a widow who already had two daughters of her own.* *At first, Cinderella thought she would gain a new family who would love her. However, things turned out differently.*

***Complication:***

*Cinderella’s new stepmother and stepsisters were cruel and selfish. While her father had to work far outside the village, Cinderella was left under their care. Every day, she was forced to do all the housework, while her stepsisters enjoyed themselves with beautiful dresses and jewelry. They treated Cinderella more like a servant than a sister.*

*Despite the hardship, Cinderella always remained kind and patient. Deep down, she hoped her father would return soon and notice her suffering.*

*One day, joyful news spread across the kingdom. The royal court officials announced that the prince would hold a grand ball at the palace. The ball was a special occasion, as the prince was searching for a bride. Every young woman in the kingdom was invited to attend.*

*Excitement filled the village. Cinderella’s stepsisters immediately prepared fancy dresses, makeup, and jewelry to impress the prince. Cinderella also wanted to join the ball, but her stepmother forbade her. She had no beautiful gown to wear, and she was ordered to stay at home to clean the house.*

*Feeling heartbroken, Cinderella cried in the corner of the kitchen. Suddenly, a warm light appeared, and a kind fairy godmother stood before her. With a gentle smile, the fairy godmother used her magic to transform Cinderella’s ragged clothes into a dazzling gown. Her worn-out shoes turned into glass slippers that sparkled under the light. A pumpkin from the garden turned into a magnificent golden carriage, and the mice in the kitchen became her loyal footmen.*

*Before sending her off, the fairy godmother gave one important warning, the magic would only last until midnight. After that, everything would return to its original state.*

*That night, Cinderella arrived at the palace. Everyone was stunned by her beauty and grace. The prince, captivated by her charm, invited her to dance. They danced together all night, forgetting the world around them. However, as the clock struck midnight, Cinderella remembered the fairy’s warning. Panicking, she rushed out of the ballroom. In her haste, one of her glass slippers slipped off and was left behind on the palace steps. The prince, desperate not to lose her, picked up the slipper. Everything else vanished, the carriage, the footmen, and the gown, but the glass slipper remained.*

***Resolution:***

*The next day, the prince announced that he would marry the woman whose foot fit the glass slipper. He ordered his men to visit every house in the kingdom. Many young women tried on the slipper, but none of them succeeded.*

*Finally, the search reached Cinderella’s home. Her stepsisters eagerly tried the slipper, but their feet were too big. The stepmother, fearing that Cinderella might succeed, tried to hide her. However, the prince insisted on meeting every maiden in the house.*

*When Cinderella appeared, she humbly tried on the slipper. To everyone’s amazement, it fit her perfectly. The prince immediately recognized her as the mysterious girl he had danced with.*

*With joy, the prince asked Cinderella to marry him. Cinderella forgave her stepmother and stepsisters for their cruelty, showing that kindness always wins in the end. She moved to the palace, married the prince, and lived happily ever after. From then on, Cinderella’s life changed forever, proving that patience, kindness, and hope can turn sorrow into happiness.*

## 7\. Contoh Narrative Text Malin Kundang

**Malin Kundang**

***Orientation:***

*Once upon a time, in a small coastal village in West Sumatra, Indonesia, lived a poor fisherman and his only son, Malin Kundang. Malin’s father had passed away when he was very young, leaving his mother to raise him alone. Despite their poverty, his mother worked tirelessly to provide food and clothes for her beloved son. Malin grew up to be a bright, strong, and ambitious young man. Deep inside, he dreamed of leaving the village one day, sailing across the seas, and becoming a wealthy and respected man.*

***Complication:***

*One morning, a large trading ship docked at the harbor near his village. Seeing the opportunity, Malin asked permission from his mother to join the sailors and chase his dream of fortune. With tears in her eyes, his mother reluctantly allowed him to go, reminding him to never forget her and his humble origins. Malin promised faithfully before departing.*

*Years went by, and Malin Kundang never returned home. He became a successful merchant, owning many ships and trading goods across islands. His wealth grew, and he married a beautiful woman of noble birth. To everyone else, he was admired as a rich and powerful man, but in his heart, Malin was too proud to look back at his poor childhood.*

*One day, while Malin’s grand ship anchored near the coast of West Sumatra, villagers recognized him and spread the news to his mother. Overjoyed, the old woman rushed to the harbor and called out his name with trembling hands waving in the air. “Malin, my son! It’s me, your mother!”, she cried in tears.*

*Instead of welcoming her, Malin Kundang felt embarrassed and angry. Afraid his noble wife and crew would discover his poor background, he denied knowing the woman. “Who is this old beggar? I have no mother like her!” he shouted. His mother begged desperately, clinging to the hope that Malin would soften his heart. But Malin, blinded by pride, pushed her away and ordered his crew to set sail immediately, leaving his mother brokenhearted on the shore.*

***Resolution:***

*Not long after, the sky turned dark and the sea grew wild. A powerful storm struck Malin Kundang’s ship, tossing it violently with massive waves. The crew panicked, but no one could control the raging sea. As lightning flashed and thunder roared, Malin stumbled on the deck, realizing too late the consequences of his arrogance.*

*Suddenly, a bolt of lightning struck him, and in an instant, Malin Kundang’s body turned stiff. His proud figure was transformed into stone, standing frozen on the deck of his ship. The waves calmed, but Malin remained as a stone statue, an eternal reminder of his disobedience and pride.*

***Re-orientation:***

*The story of Malin Kundang teaches us the importance of respecting and honoring our parents, no matter how poor or humble their background may be. Wealth and success are meaningless if they lead us to arrogance and ungratefulness. Disobedience and pride, as shown in Malin’s fate, will only bring disaster in the end.*

## 8\. Contoh Narrative Text tentang Pengalaman Liburan

***My Vacation in Bali***

***Orientation:***

*Last semester break, I went on vacation to Bali, Indonesia. It was my very first time visiting the beautiful island, and I couldn’t hide my excitement. I had often heard about Bali’s amazing beaches, vibrant culture, and friendly people, so this trip felt like a dream come true.*

*When I arrived, I checked into a cozy hotel in the bustling area of Kuta, just a short walk from the famous Kuta Beach. From the balcony of my room, I could see the golden sands and the sparkling waves of the Indian Ocean. At night, I could even hear the sound of waves crashing, which made me feel both relaxed and thrilled to start my adventure.*

***Complication:***

*The first day, I explored the local markets filled with colorful stalls selling handmade crafts, batik clothes, and unique souvenirs. I couldn’t resist trying some local food, like spicy chicken satay served with peanut sauce, and fresh tropical smoothies made from mangoes and pineapples. The flavors were unforgettable!*

*On the second day, I joined a tour to Ubud, famous for its traditional culture and iconic rice terraces. The drive was breathtaking, lush green rice fields stretched endlessly, while tall palm trees lined the road. When I finally arrived at the terraces, I was mesmerized by the view. I walked along narrow paths, took dozens of photos, and even chatted with a local farmer who kindly explained how rice is planted and harvested.*

*Later in the week, I decided to try snorkeling at one of Bali’s coral reefs. The water was warm and crystal clear, allowing me to see schools of colorful fish swimming gracefully among the corals. I even spotted a small sea turtle gliding peacefully. It felt like stepping into another world under the sea, and I didn’t want to leave.*

*Of course, I also spent some lazy afternoons at the beach. I rented a beach chair, enjoyed the sunshine, and read my favorite book while sipping a cold coconut drink. In the evenings, I watched the stunning sunset, orange, pink, and purple colors painted the sky as surfers rode the last waves of the day.*

***Resolution:***

*By the end of my trip, I realized I had spent more money than I expected on souvenirs, tours, and food. But honestly, I didn’t regret it at all. Every moment I experienced in Bali was priceless, from the beauty of nature to the warmth of the people.*

*My vacation in Bali was truly unforgettable, and it gave me memories that I will cherish forever. I learned to appreciate not only the natural wonders but also the culture and hospitality of the Balinese people. One day, I definitely want to come back and explore even more of what this magical island has to offer.*

## 9\. Contoh Narrative Text Rapunzel

**Rapunzel**

***Orientation:***

*Once upon a time, in a kingdom far, far away, there lived a kind couple who longed for a child more than anything. After many years of waiting and praying, their wish was finally granted, the wife became pregnant.*

*During her pregnancy, she craved a special kind of herb called rapunzel, which was said to have magical healing properties. With great care and joy, the couple prepared for the baby’s arrival. Finally, the wife gave birth to a beautiful baby girl, and they named her Rapunzel, after the herb that had saved her life.*

***Complication:***

*Unfortunately, the herb Rapunzel needed grew only in the garden of a wicked witch who lived nearby. Desperate to help his wife, the husband sneaked into the witch’s garden at night to steal some. But the witch caught him red-handed.*

*The witch’s eyes gleamed with anger, yet she offered a deal,* *“You may take all the herbs you want, but in return, you must give me your baby when it is born.”*

*Terrified and without thinking of the consequences, the husband agreed. When the baby was born, the witch came and took her away.*

*She locked Rapunzel in a tall, lonely tower deep in the forest. The tower had no stairs or doors, only a single window at the very top. Rapunzel grew up there, isolated from the world, with only the witch as her visitor.*

*Every day, the witch would stand at the bottom of the tower and call out,* *“Rapunzel, Rapunzel, let down your hair!”.* *Rapunzel’s golden hair was so long and strong that the witch could climb it like a rope to reach the window.*

*As years passed, Rapunzel’s singing voice became more beautiful, filling the forest with melodies that touched every heart. One day, a young prince riding nearby heard the enchanting song. Curious, he followed the voice until he found the tower.* *He hid and observed how the witch called Rapunzel. Later, when the witch had gone, he tried it himself,* *“Rapunzel, Rapunzel, let down your hair!”*

*To his amazement, the hair came tumbling down, and he climbed up. When Rapunzel saw him, she was startled at first, but soon they began to talk. They laughed, shared stories, and eventually fell deeply in love.*

*For many days, the prince visited Rapunzel in secret. But one day, the witch discovered their meetings. Furious, she cut off Rapunzel’s golden hair and banished her to a faraway desert. Then, she tricked the prince with Rapunzel’s hair. When he climbed up expecting to see her, the witch pushed him from the tower. He fell into the thorns below and lost his sight.*

***Resolution:***

*The prince wandered through the forest, blind and heartbroken, searching for his beloved Rapunzel. He lived for years in sorrow, guided only by the hope of hearing her voice again.*

*One day, the prince heard a familiar melody carried by the wind. His heart leaped with joy. Following the sound, he finally found Rapunzel. Overwhelmed with emotion, she embraced him and cried tears of happiness. Her tears fell upon his eyes, and miraculously, his sight was restored.* *Together with their children, Rapunzel and the prince returned to his kingdom, where they lived happily ever after.*

## 10\. Contoh Narrative Text The Haunted House

***The Haunted House***

***Orientation:***

*On the outskirts of a small quiet town, stood an old abandoned house that everyone feared. Its wooden walls were rotting, the windows were broken, and thick vines covered the roof. People said it was haunted, those who dared to enter were never seen again. At night, strange noises, whispers, and even screams could be heard echoing from inside. Because of its terrifying reputation, no one dared to go near the house, especially after sunset.*

***Complication:***

*One summer evening, a group of four adventurous teenagers, Liam, Sarah, Jake, and Emily, decided to challenge the town’s legend. Carrying flashlights and their phones, they entered the house with a mix of fear and excitement.*

*Inside, the air was cold and heavy, and the floor creaked with every step. Cobwebs hung from the ceiling, and the smell of damp wood filled the air. In one of the dusty rooms, they discovered an old diary. Curious, Sarah opened it and read aloud. The diary belonged to a family who had once lived in the house decades ago. The entries revealed a dark secret, the family had been brutally murdered by a deranged relative who had secretly lived in the basement.*

*Suddenly, the sound of footsteps echoed through the hall. The teenagers froze. A whisper followed, “Get out…!!!”. They turned to leave, but when Jake tried to open the door, it wouldn’t budge. The windows were sealed shut, and every exit was locked. Panic began to set in. The teenagers realized they were trapped inside the haunted house, just like the people in the stories.*

***Resolution:***

*Hours passed, and the night grew darker. The ghosts of the murdered family appeared one by one, their faces pale and filled with sorrow. The teenagers screamed as the spirits forced them to relive the brutal night of the murders, hearing the cries, feeling the terror, and witnessing the horrific scene over and over again.*

*By morning, the house fell silent once more. When the townspeople came to check, they found no sign of the teenagers. Only the diary lying open on the floor. Their phones and flashlights were gone, as if they had never been there.*

*To this day, locals believe the teenagers are still trapped inside, doomed to repeat the horrors of the house alongside the spirits of the murdered family. The haunted house remains abandoned, standing as a chilling reminder that some legends are not just stories, they are warnings.*

## 11\. Contoh Narrative Text Lutung Kasarung

**Lutung Kasarung**

***Orientation:***

*Once upon a time, in the Sundanese kingdom of Pasir Batang, there lived a beautiful princess named Purbasari Ayuwangi. She was kind, gentle, and blessed with a melodious singing voice that could soothe anyone who heard it. However, her beauty and talent caused jealousy in her stepmother, Galuh, and her stepsister, Purbararang, who wanted the throne for themselves.*

*Because of their envy, they falsely accused Purbasari of bringing misfortune to the kingdom. The king, misled by these lies, banished Purbasari to a deep forest. Lonely and heartbroken, she wandered in despair, until she met a mysterious monkey named Lutung Kasarung. Unlike ordinary monkeys, Lutung Kasarung possessed kindness, wisdom, and a magical aura. He became Purbasari’s loyal friend and comforted her during her exile.*

***Complication:***

*Life in the forest was not easy, but with Lutung Kasarung’s help, Purbasari survived. One day, Lutung Kasarung revealed his magical powers by creating a beautiful garden and a crystal-clear lake for her. Slowly, news of her beauty and her enchanting voice spread beyond the forest.*

*A noble prince, Prince Raden Indrajaya, heard about Purbasari’s charm and sought her out. Upon meeting, he was captivated by her kindness and her heavenly singing. They soon fell in love, and Purbasari’s life began to brighten again.*

*However, when Purbararang and her mother discovered Purbasari’s happiness, their jealousy burned stronger than ever. They plotted to kill her. One night, they ambushed Purbasari while she was walking near the lake. Lutung Kasarung bravely fought to protect her, but in the struggle, he was gravely wounded.*

*At the moment of his death, something miraculous happened. Lutung Kasarung transformed into his true form, a handsome celestial prince cursed to live as a monkey. With his final strength, he saved Purbasari from her stepmother’s evil scheme.*

***Resolution:***

*After the truth came to light, Purbararang and her mother were punished for their cruelty. Purbasari returned to the kingdom and was crowned as queen, ruling with wisdom and compassion.*

*To honor Lutung Kasarung’s sacrifice, Purbasari built a statue of him in the royal garden. The statue, blessed with his celestial powers, became a symbol of protection and prosperity for the kingdom. The people believed that as long as the statue stood, their land would remain safe and abundant.*

***Re-orientation:***

*The tale of Lutung Kasarung carries deep meaning in Indonesian, especially Sundanese, culture. It reminds us of the strength of friendship, loyalty, and true love, as well as the courage to stand against injustice and envy. The story also reflects the belief that kindness and sincerity will always triumph over evil.*

*Passed down from generation to generation, Lutung Kasarung remains a beloved folktale that not only entertains but also teaches important values about life, sacrifice, and integrity.*

## 12\. Contoh Narrative Text Robin Hood and The Golden Arrow

***Robin Hood and The Golden Arrow***

***Orientation:***

*Once upon a time, in the heart of Sherwood Forest, lived a legendary outlaw named Robin Hood. He was famous throughout the land as a skilled archer and the fearless leader of his loyal companions, Little John, Friar Tuck, Will Scarlet, and Alan-a-Dale. Together, they formed the Merry Men, a band of brave fighters who stood up against the cruelty of the Sheriff of Nottingham and the greed of corrupt nobles.*

*Robin Hood and his Merry Men were loved by the common folk because they had a noble mission, stealing from the rich to give to the poor. Their clever tricks, daring raids, and remarkable skills in archery made them both heroes and enemies of the kingdom.*

***Complication:***

*One day, the Sheriff of Nottingham grew restless. Despite all his efforts, he had never been able to capture Robin Hood. So, he devised a cunning plan. He announced a grand archery contest in the town square, with a glittering golden arrow as the prize. The Sheriff hoped the lure of the prize would be irresistible to Robin Hood, drawing him out of hiding so he could finally be caught.*

*Robin Hood, upon hearing the news, immediately sensed it was a trap. Yet, his love for archery and his daring spirit pushed him to accept the challenge. He smiled and said to his Merry Men, “This is the perfect chance to show the Sheriff that he cannot outsmart me.”*

*On the day of the contest, the town was buzzing with excitement. Skilled archers from all corners of the land gathered to prove their ability. Robin Hood, clever as always, disguised himself as an old beggar, wearing ragged clothes and carrying a simple bow. The Sheriff sat proudly in his chair, surrounded by guards, watching carefully for any sign of the outlaw.*

*As the contest began, one by one, the archers stepped forward. Some hit the target, some missed entirely, and a few came close to the bullseye. But none could make the perfect shot. Then came the turn of the “old beggar.” With steady hands and sharp eyes, Robin Hood drew his bow. The crowd held their breath as he released the arrow—thwack!—it landed directly in the center of the target, splitting the bullseye in two. The audience erupted in cheers.*

***Resolution:***

*The Sheriff’s eyes widened. He knew at once that no one but Robin Hood could shoot with such precision. He leapt from his chair and shouted, “Seize that man! That is Robin Hood in disguise!”*

*At that moment, Robin Hood pulled back his hood, revealing his true identity. Panic spread through the Sheriff’s men as cheers grew louder from the townsfolk who supported Robin Hood. Suddenly, from among the crowd, the Merry Men appeared. Little John swung his massive staff, Friar Tuck knocked guards to the ground, and Will Scarlet slashed through ropes to clear an escape path.*

*A fierce battle broke out, but Robin and his men fought bravely and managed to push back the Sheriff’s guards. In the chaos, Robin Hood grabbed the golden arrow, the prize of the contest, and with a quick whistle, he and his Merry Men vanished back into Sherwood Forest.*

*From that day, the tale of Robin Hood’s daring victory at the golden arrow contest spread across villages and towns. The common people laughed at how Robin once again outsmarted the Sheriff, and they admired his bravery even more. His story became a symbol of hope, justice, and courage, reminding everyone that even in times of oppression, heroes could rise to defend the poor and powerless.*

## 13\. Contoh Narrative Text Danau Toba

**Danau Toba**

***Orientation:***

*Long ago, in the land of North Sumatra, there lived a man named Batara Guru. He was a humble farmer who worked hard every day in the rice fields. One day, Batara Guru met a beautiful mortal woman named Ompu Ni Bolon. They fell deeply in love and later got married. From their marriage, they had a son named Batara Sihir, who was no ordinary child. He was born with mysterious supernatural powers that made him different from other children in the village.*

***Complication:***

*One afternoon, while fishing in the river, Batara Sihir caught an unusual fish. The fish had shimmering golden scales and glowing eyes, unlike any fish he had ever seen. Curious and hungry, Batara Sihir brought the fish home and tried to cook it.*

*To his surprise, before the fish was fully cooked, it transformed into a beautiful woman named Boru Toba. She revealed that she was a magical creature cursed to live as a fish until someone discovered her true form. Batara Sihir was captivated by her beauty and kindness, and soon they fell in love. They married and were blessed with a son named Partomuan.*

*However, their happiness did not last long. The gods grew angry at Batara Sihir’s union with Boru Toba, for it was forbidden for humans to marry magical beings. As punishment, the gods decided to curse Batara Sihir and his family. One stormy night, thunder roared and the earth shook violently. Batara Sihir, Boru Toba, and their son were turned into stone statues.*

***Resolution:***

*The place where the stone family stood collapsed, leaving a massive hole in the ground. Rain poured endlessly, filling the hole with water. Over time, this became a vast and beautiful lake, which is now known as Danau Toba (Lake Toba).*

***Re-orientation:***

*The legend of Danau Toba has been passed down from generation to generation, especially among the Batak people of North Sumatra. It carries a powerful message, the importance of respecting the natural world and the dangers of disobeying the will of the gods. Today, Lake Toba is not only a symbol of natural beauty but also a reminder of cultural heritage and moral values deeply rooted in Indonesian folklore.*

## 14\. Contoh Narrative Text Roro Jonggrang

**Roro Jonggrang**

***Orientation:***

*Long time ago, in the heart of Central Java, there lived a powerful king named Prabu Boko. He was a giant king who ruled with strength and authority. However, his greed and ambition knew no bounds. One day, Prabu Boko and his soldiers attacked the neighboring kingdom of Prambanan, ruled by King Prabu Damar Moyo. After a fierce battle, Prabu Boko was defeated and killed by the mighty Prince Bandung Bondowoso, the son of King Damar Moyo.*

*Victorious and powerful, Bandung Bondowoso took control of Prambanan. While exploring the defeated kingdom, he laid his eyes upon Roro Jonggrang, the beautiful daughter of Prabu Boko. Struck by her beauty, Bandung Bondowoso immediately fell in love and proposed marriage.*

***Complication:***

*Roro Jonggrang was horrified. She hated Bandung Bondowoso for killing her father, and she had no desire to marry him. But she also knew that refusing a mighty prince like him could bring disaster to her people. Thinking quickly, Roro Jonggrang devised a clever plan. She set forth an impossible condition.*

*She said, “I will marry you, Bandung Bondowoso, if you can build me a thousand temples in just one night.”*

*Confident in his supernatural powers, Bandung Bondowoso agreed. With the help of his spirit soldiers and a powerful demon army, he began building the temples at lightning speed. By midnight, hundreds of temples had already stood tall, and before dawn, only one temple was left unfinished.*

*Seeing the temples nearly completed, Roro Jonggrang grew anxious. If Bandung Bondowoso succeeded, she would have no choice but to marry him. In desperation, she gathered the villagers and ordered them to pound rice, light torches, and create noises that resembled the coming of dawn.*

***Resolution:***

*The spirits, thinking the sun was about to rise, panicked and fled, leaving the last temple unfinished. Bandung Bondowoso realized that he had been tricked by Roro Jonggrang. Furious with her deception, he cursed her on the spot,* *“You have fooled me, Roro Jonggrang! Very well, if you want a thousand temples, let this final one be you!”.*

*Instantly, Roro Jonggrang was turned into stone. Her figure became the final statue, completing the thousand temples that today are known as Candi Sewu, near Prambanan Temple in Yogyakarta.*

***Re-orientation:***

*The legend of Roro Jonggrang remains one of the most famous folktales in Javanese culture. It is a story that reflects the danger of deceit, the consequences of broken promises, and the power of wit against brute force. Until now, the statue believed to be Roro Jonggrang can still be seen inside Prambanan Temple, serving as a timeless reminder of this tale.*

## 15\. Contoh Narrative Text The Story of Hercules

***The Story of Hercules***

***Orientation:***

*Hercules was one of the most famous heroes in Greek mythology, admired for his incredible strength, bravery, and perseverance. He was the son of Zeus, the king of the gods, and a mortal woman named Alcmene. This made him half-god and half-human, which gave him extraordinary abilities beyond ordinary men. From a young age, Hercules showed signs of his divine strength. He was said to have strangled two serpents sent to kill him while still in his cradle.*

***Complication:***

*However, Hercules’s life was far from peaceful. Hera, the wife of Zeus, hated him deeply because he was the illegitimate son of her husband. Out of jealousy and spite, Hera constantly tried to make his life miserable.*

*One day, Hera cast a spell of madness upon Hercules. In his insanity, he tragically killed his beloved wife, Megara, and their children. When he regained his senses, Hercules was horrified and filled with unbearable grief and guilt. His sorrow was so great that he wished to punish himself and seek redemption for his terrible actions.*

***Resolution:***

*To atone for his sins, the Oracle of Delphi instructed Hercules to serve King Eurystheus of Tiryns and perform twelve nearly impossible tasks, which later became known as the Twelve Labors of Hercules. Each labor tested not only his strength but also his courage and endurance.*

*First, he confronted the fearsome Nemean Lion, a beast whose skin no weapon could pierce. With his bare hands, Hercules wrestled the lion and eventually defeated it, proving his unmatched power.* *Not long after, he was tasked with capturing the Golden Hind of Artemis, a sacred and swift deer that could outrun any hunter. Hercules pursued it tirelessly across mountains and forests until he finally caught the creature alive.*

*One of his most grueling tasks came when he had to clean the Augean Stables in just a single day. These stables housed thousands of cattle and had not been cleaned for many years. Using his wit as well as his strength, Hercules diverted a river to wash away the filth in record time.*

*He also fought against the deadly Lernaean Hydra, a serpent-like monster with many heads that grew back each time one was cut off. With the help of his nephew Iolaus, Hercules managed to burn the necks of the Hydra, preventing the heads from regenerating.*

*His challenges did not stop there. Hercules hunted down and captured the Erymanthian Boar, wrestled the mighty Cretan Bull, and even ventured to the edge of the world to retrieve the Apples of the Hesperides, which were guarded by a terrifying dragon.*

*Despite the dangers, Hercules completed all twelve labors successfully. Each challenge he overcame brought him closer to redemption and earned him eternal fame among mortals and gods alike.*

*After enduring years of hardship, Hercules finally found peace. His heroic deeds not only redeemed him from his past mistakes but also secured his place as a legendary figure in Greek mythology. In the end, the gods honored him by granting him immortality and a place among them on Mount Olympus.*

***Re-orientation:***

*From his story, we can learn that even the greatest heroes make mistakes, but true greatness lies in facing those mistakes, seeking redemption, and using one’s strength for good.*
